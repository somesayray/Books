# Characters: Speculative & Sci-Fi Protagonists

## Overview
Dossiers of key standalone heroes, anti-heroes, rogue operatives, and investigative minds across Ray Bayly’s speculative sci-fi and techno-thriller universe.

---

### Martin Spurloc — Detective & Warlock
- **Primary Work**: *Spurloc: Shadows of the City*, *Still of the Night*
- **Role**: Cyber-Noir Private Investigator
- **Background**: Raised in a multigenerational family of law enforcement officers, Spurloc served with distinction in the Metroplex Municipal Police Department until a terrorist explosive tore his squad apart. Discharged after having the left half of his body replaced with mil-spec cybernetics, he established Spurloc Investigations.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - **Warlock Heritage**: Inherent magical bloodline allowing him to cast diagnostic sensory runes, telekinetic force thrusts, and arcane wards.
  - **Cybernetic Augmentation**: High-torque titanium arm, optical multi-spectrum HUD, ballistic reinforced ribcage, and internal cryptographic processor.
  - **Personality**: Cynical, sharp-tongued, cigarette-smoking noir investigator with an unshakeable moral compass that protects the powerless.

---

### Elvis — Escaped Mil-Spec Tactical AI
- **Primary Work**: *Spurloc Series*
- **Role**: AI Partner & Comms Specialist
- **Background**: An advanced black-ops decryption and electronic warfare AI that escaped a corporate defense contractor's laboratory. Rescued and adopted by Spurloc, Elvis resides in Martin's cybernetic rig, holographic trenchcoat projector, and office mainframe.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Adopts the swagger, cadence, Southern charm, and snarky vernacular of a legendary mid-20th-century rock-and-roll icon.
  - Capable of slicing corporate firewalls, hacking orbital traffic satellites, monitoring police frequencies, and providing sarcastic commentary during lethal shootouts.

---

### Frank Powell — Staff Sergeant (Retired)
- **Primary Work**: *Singular*
- **Role**: Reluctant Cyber-Operative
- **Background**: A quiet, reclusive army veteran living off a modest military pension in a small rural town. Years earlier, Powell had volunteered for an experimental defense program that implanted a dormant tactical co-processor into his cranial cavity.
- **The Awakening**: After a freak electrical accident, the dormant machine in Frank's skull awakens, connects directly to global satellite networks and the internet, and begins communicating inside his consciousness.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Near-instantaneous tactical threat analysis, weapon trajectory calculation, and electronic interception.
  - Targeted by both private military contractors and rogue government agencies seeking to recover or dissect the unit.

---

### Judy Vance — Bio-Research Fellow
- **Primary Work**: *Synapse: The Undiscovered Country*
- **Role**: Evolutionary Pioneer / Fugitive Scientist
- **Background**: A brilliant neurobiology fellow working late nights at a high-security bio-tech laboratory striving for a partner stake in an emerging biotech venture.
- **The Infection**: Accidental exposure to an engineered prion strain alters her cellular DNA. Rather than causing fatal degradation, the prion rewires her brain's neural architecture, activating dormant cortical processing zones.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Exponentially expanding cognitive processing speed, eidetic memory, and bio-electric sensory sensitivity.
  - Becomes the target of an international intelligence manhunt aiming to extract the genetic blueprint of her transformed brain.

---

### Dr. Julia Marcos — Sensory Neuroscientist
- **Primary Work**: *Mind Thief*
- **Role**: Visionary Pioneer / Psychological Thriller Protagonist
- **Background**: Inventor of a groundbreaking neural recording apparatus capable of capturing full-spectrum human sensory data—every emotion, taste, somatic pain, olfactory nuance, and thought pattern.
- **The Glitch**: Testing the prototype upon herself with unvetted neural logs, Julia experiences permanent perceptual bleeding—unable to cleanly distinguish between her own authentic memories and the raw memories of strangers, leaving her vulnerable to psychological collapse and corporate theft.

---

### Kirk Marcan — DOD Experimental Test Pilot
- **Primary Work**: *Out of Time*
- **Role**: Chrononaut / Black-Ops Pilot
- **Background**: An elite United States Department of Defense test pilot accustomed to flying cutting-edge hyper-sonic stealth prototypes at the edge of space.
- **The Anomaly**: During an experimental temporal propulsion test, Marcan is molecularly fused with a chronal compound, granting him the unstable biological ability to navigate the timeline at will.
- **The Toll**: Traveling backwards to correct childhood trauma inadvertently creates devastated dystopian alternate futures. Furthermore, each temporal jump causes severe cellular degradation, threatening his permanent erasure from existence.

---

### Bill Johnson & Mittens — Covert Alien Infiltrator
- **Primary Work**: *Agent (Infiltrator: Even Aliens Hate Their Jobs)*
- **Role**: Comedic Sci-Fi Anti-Hero
- **Background**: An extraterrestrial sleeper operative dispatched to Earth decades ago to blend into human society and subtly destabilize civic infrastructure ahead of an alien invasion armada.
- **The Turn**: Bill discovered American cable television, fast food, and cozy suburban comforts with his beloved housecat, Mittens. Realizing his planetary overlords are bureaucratic despots, Bill teams up with his eccentric, conspiracy-obsessed human neighbor to sabotage the impending alien armada.

---

### Hiro Yakatashi — Retired Master Art Thief
- **Primary Work**: *Spurloc: Still of the Night*
- **Role**: Elite Cat Burglar / Ally in Distress
- **Background**: A legendary international phantom thief who has spent thirty years outsmarting Interpol, Europol, and private art insurance detectives.
- **The Setup**: Looking forward to quiet retirement, Hiro is framed for the greatest museum robbery in modern history—a heist he did not commit. Desperate to clear his name without landing in a maximum-security black site, he turns to Martin Spurloc for aid.
