# Characters: Sanguine Legacy & The Supernatural Compact

## Overview
Dossiers of mortal heirs, ancient vampires, lycanthrope alphas, covert witches, and Vatican relic custodians battling across modern urban sprawl and dark supernatural feudal realms.

---

## 1. Sanguine Relics & Sanguine Lords

### The Sanguine Heir (Victor Vance / Modern Descendant)
- **Role**: Protagonist (*Legacy: Sanguine Relics — Book 1*)
- **Background**: An unassuming American park ranger and dedicated family man living in Maryland, unaware that his genealogical lineage traces directly to King Ferdinand III of Castile (1199–1252).
- **The Awakening**: When a brutal werewolf attack in Great Falls draws the Franciscan Order to his doorstep, rogue wolves attack his home. To protect his wife Jessica and their children (Lucas and Emma), Victor agrees to enter protective exile and takes possession of the iron chest of Castile.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Through the Jamestown training dagger, absorbs ten years of combat muscle memory from Alfonso of Castile within hours.
  - Wields *Lobera* ("The Wolf-Slayer"), mastering the ancient Aramaic command words (*Bakhzataa*, *Malka*, *Shlama*) to project kinetic silver arcs and bio-thermal aura vision.
  - Establishes the New Covenant, rising to become the Sovereign Arbiter between humanity and the supernatural.

---

### Alfonso of Castile / Sowain — The Elder Alpha
- **Role**: Co-Protagonist & Mentor (*Legacy / The Pack*)
- **Background**: Direct grand-nephew of King Ferdinand III. In the 17th century, he was the Order's greatest Hunter, deployed to the Jamestown colony in 1612. After realizing the wolves were peaceful guardians rather than demons, he refused to massacre a native den, took the bite to preserve peace, and adopted the name Sowain.
- **The Modern Era**: Has guided the Rockville pack for over two centuries under a strict code of peaceful coexistence. Crosses the Atlantic to Scotland to prevent Brock's betrayal and protect Victor.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Colossal 7-foot jet-black alpha form with centuries of martial experience blending royal Toledo swordsmanship with apex predatory power.
  - Bound by blood and honor to Victor as a spiritual uncle and guardian.

---

### Father Edgar MacIntyre — Scottish Knight of the Order
- **Role**: Combat Mentor (*Legacy*)
- **Background**: A rugged, whisky-drinking Scottish Franciscan priest and veteran Knight of the Custodes stationed at an isolated estate outside Dundee.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Master swordsman and tactical shotgun expert who trains Victor in the live-steel fundamentals of *Lobera*.
  - Possesses unwavering moral integrity; rebels against the extremists in Rome when he uncovers Captain Mercer's plot to commit biological genocide.

---

### Captain Thomas Mercer — Commander of the Custodes Sanguinis
- **Role**: Antagonist (*Legacy*)
- **Background**: The ruthless, fanatical military commander of the Order's strike divisions. Rejects theological moderation and views all supernaturals as vermin to be exterminated.
- **Conspiracy**: Developed "Project Argentum"—weaponized aerosol micro-silver designed to trigger acute pulmonary necrosis across entire geographic glens. Remanded to Vatican custody following Victor's victory at Glen Clova.

---

### Rebecca — Pack Scout & Tracker
- **Role**: Ally (*Legacy / The Pack*)
- **Background**: A swift, highly observant scout in the Rockville pack who initially surveils Victor in Maryland, noting his decency and love for his family. Later acts as the medical healer and vanguard during the Scottish campaign.

---

### Brock — Rogue Berserker Alpha
- **Role**: Antagonist (*Legacy*)
- **Background**: An aggressive, power-hungry wolf in the Rockville pack who rejects Sowain's peaceful treaties. Allies with the conspirator Martin, bombs the Rockville garage, and attacks Victor's home. Slain by Victor in the crypts of Saint Jude's Monastery.

---

### Marius — Betrayed Outrider Commander
- **Role**: Protagonist (*Outrider: Reign of the Sanguine Lords*)
- **Background**: For over a century, Marius served as the elite strike leader of Sanguine Lord Artemis’s personal special forces, waging brutal territory wars across the ruined post-fall seaboard.
- **The Betrayal**: Deemed too popular and dangerous by Lord Artemis, Marius was betrayed and executed by two of his closest lieutenants, who dumped his body in the toxic ruins of derelict Baltimore.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Ancient vampire physiology enhanced by decades of militarized trench and urban warfare.
  - Saved by a cynical, foul-mouthed underground doctor and taken in by "The Strays"—a resistance faction of rogue supernaturals and unenslaved mortal scavengers.
  - Driven by cold, methodical retribution against Artemis and the corrupt Sanguine aristocracy.

---

### Lord Artemis — Overlord of the Eastern Sanguine Enclave
- **Role**: Antagonist (*Sanguine Lords / Outrider*)
- **Background**: A ruthless, narcissistic vampire lord who seized dominion over the ruins of the Mid-Atlantic corridor, establishing a feudal empire of mortal blood-farms and conscripted thrall legions.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Aristocratic sadist with overwhelming telepathic domination powers and blood-magic regeneration.
  - Paranoiac obsession with retaining supreme dominance, executing any lieutenant showing independent thought or tactical brilliance.

## 2. Dark Council: Team Beta (Boston)

### Marcus — The Vampire Enforcer
- **Role**: Team Lead / Investigator (*The Compact*)
- **Background**: A centuries-old vampire nobleman bound by honor to the Dark Council. Chosen to head Team Beta—the Boston Special Branch tasked with upholding "The Compact."
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Superhuman kinetic speed, silent movement, hypnotic gaze, and centuries of deductive investigation experience.
  - Pragmatic and aristocratic, he maintains an iron grip on supernatural disputes to ensure humanity remains oblivious to the darkness.

---

### Evelyn — The Thaumaturgical Witch
- **Role**: Arcane Investigator & Spellweaver
- **Background**: Inheritor of a prominent Salem witch lineage. Acts as the magical forensics specialist for Team Beta.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Master of warding circles, blood tracking, memory extraction, and elemental shielding.
  - Senses occult residue and breaches of dimensional barriers left by illicit dark magic covens.

---

### Asher — The Werewolf Tracker
- **Role**: Heavy Muscle & Bloodhound
- **Background**: A fierce lycanthrope exiled from traditional pack politics due to his belief in the rule of law and inter-species peace under The Compact.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Unrivaled olfactory and auditory tracking capabilities; capable of smelling blood, adrenaline, and gunpowder from miles away.
  - Devastating physical resilience, capable of shifting into full battle-form during high-threat engagements with rogue abominations.
