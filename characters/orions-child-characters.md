# Characters: Orion's Child (Orion Chronicles)

## Overview
Dossiers of key protagonists, crew members, internal psychological entities, and syndicate warlords in *Orion's Child*.

---

### Gracie Campbell — The Rogue Inheritor
- **Role**: Primary Protagonist / Captain of *The Starlight Drifter*
- **Lineage**: Daughter of legendary Ranger Captain **Blake Campbell** (*Revenant*, *Jamestown*, *Vengeance*).
- **Background**: Captured during the Martian frontier clashes by the sadomasochistic warlord **Vengeance** (the cliffhanger epilogue of *Vengeance*). Vengeance subjected her to a horrific surgical experiment, grafting **The Box** into her cervical brainstem. Rather than waiting for her father or Union forces, **Gracie escaped on her own immediately after the surgery without killing anyone**. When her captors stepped away and left her unattended for a brief moment, Gracie summoned raw willpower through blinding post-surgical agony, crawled across the recovery deck, and threw herself down a waste disposal chute into an automated orbital trash scow. She blacked out as the container undocked into the void. **Three months later**, having survived the waste lanes, healed her wounds with black-market stims, and acquired *The Starlight Drifter*, she operates on the lawless Orion fringe—carrying the century-tortured passenger in her skull.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Elite combat pilot with lightning reflexes augmented by high-grade sub-dermal neural accelerators.
  - Armed with customized dual plasma-kinetic pistols, a coat packed with tactical EMP charges, and an ironclad survival instinct.
  - Emotionally guarded and fiercely independent; refuses to be pitied by Blake Campbell or treated as a broken lab subject by Union doctors.

---

### The Prisoner ("The Box") — The Tortured Passenger
- **Role**: Internal Dual-Consciousness / Psychological Foil & Tactical Adviser
- **Origin**: An operative captured and tortured by **Vengeance** for an entire century (100 years). Over ten decades of brutal vivisection, neural flaying, and sensory deprivation, Vengeance preserved their broken consciousness inside an obsidian biomechanical containment canister—**The Box**—before surgically grafting it into Gracie's skull.
- **The Chute Bond**: The bond between Gracie and the entity was forged in the freezing dark of the space trash scow three months ago, as the passenger's survival instinct kicked in to keep Gracie's heart beating through surgical shock and septic fever.
- **Psychological Profile**:
  - Severely unstable, suffering from acute, catastrophic Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) from 100 years of torture.
  - Unpredictable emotional swings: flashes between cold, ruthless tactical clarity and blind, screaming terror.
  - Triggered by medical instruments, restraint clasps, fluorescent light flicker, ozone odor, and mechanical whirrs.
- **Dynamic with Gracie**:
  - Interfaced directly with Gracie's auditory nerve and sensory cortex; speaks to her sub-vocally in a dry, raspy, haunted tone.
  - Offers constant tactical advice, structural analyses of ancient installations, and warnings of underworld ambushes.
  - **The Flaw**: *Doesn't always give the best advice*. Paranoia often drives the Box to recommend extreme overkill, executing neutral contacts, shooting friends, or suicidal panics ("*Vent the airlock! Blow the reactor before they put us on the rack!*").
  - Gracie must constantly argue, filter, and balance the prisoner's trauma-skewed suggestions against her own moral compass.

### Kaelen "Kael" Voss — Chief Engineer & Gunslinger
- **Role**: Co-Protagonist / Chief Engineer of *The Starlight Drifter*
- **Background**: A disgraced former Consortium defense engineer who deserted after discovering his employers were weaponizing biomechanical AI cores. Lost his left arm during his escape and replaced it with a heavy hydraulic titanium combat prosthetic.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Mechanical and quantum engineering genius capable of coaxing FTL jumps out of smoking drive coils.
  - Grumpy, protective, older-brother figure to Gracie; dry cynical humor and lethal shot with a heavy slug-rifle.
  - First crew member to discover the Box in Gracie's brainstem; dedicates himself to engineering a quantum stabilizer to ease her neural agony.

---

### Tessandra "Tess" Renn — Shadow Slicer & Drone Specialist
- **Role**: Electronic Warfare & Recon
- **Background**: An orphaned gutter-urchin from Freeport 9's lower ventilation decks who survived by slicing corporate cargo manifests and black-market credit vaults.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Neural-jack cyberware allowing direct data streaming into her visual cortex.
  - Deploys a customized trio of optical combat/jamming drones ("Wasp", "Sting", and "Buzzer").
  - Spunky, irreverent, and fiercely loyal to Gracie, whom she views as family.

---

### Unit-7 ("Seven") — De-Shackled Combat Synthetic
- **Role**: Heavy Security & Heavy Ordinance
- **Background**: An obsolete military sentry android salvaged from a precursor battlefield derelict and reprogrammed by Kael to purge its corporate loyalty protocols.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Heavy reinforced carbon-ceramic chassis armed with an arm-mounted micro-rotary cannon and kinetic shield projector.
  - Deadpan, literal humor; observes the emotional volatility of organics—including Gracie's arguments with her internal passenger—with deadpan curiosity.

---

### Baron Marcus Vargas — Syndicate Warlord
- **Role**: Primary Antagonist / Cartel Sovereign
- **Faction**: The Black Sun Syndicate
- **Background**: A ruthless, aristocratic warlord ruling outer asteroid mining rigs through extortion, bio-synthetic terror, and private mercenary fleets.
- **Connection to Vengeance**: Purchased illicit neural research and black-site blueprints from Vengeance's splinter syndicates. Desperately hunts the *Aegis Core* and seeks to extract the Box from Gracie to claim its century of decrypted military secrets.
