# Characters: Shadow War Chronicles

## Overview
Dossiers of fleet admirals, starship captains, frontline marines, and alien shadow commanders during the interstellar war between humanity's Sol Defense Fleet and the extraterrestrial Incursion swarm.

---

### Captain Jaxen Vance — Commander of the *Revenant*
- **Role**: Primary Protagonist / Fleet Tactician
- **Vessel**: *UEF Revenant* (Battlecruiser-turned-flagship of the Renegades Strike Wing)
- **Background**: A battle-hardened naval commander who survived the catastrophic opening orbital siege of Mars. Refusing unconditional surrender orders, Vance rallied remnants of shattered battle groups to form an autonomous strike group.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Renowned for unconventional, high-risk void maneuvers, slipstream micro-jumps, and predatory ambush tactics.
  - Deeply loyal to his crew; carries the psychological weight of thousands lost during the early years of the Incursion.
  - Tactical specialty: Synchronizing kinetic railgun broadsides with targeted electronic warfare to cripple larger alien dreadnoughts.

---

### Commander Eric Stride — Tactical Officer of the *Yorktown*
- **Role**: Co-Protagonist / Gunnery Specialist
- **Vessel**: *UEF Yorktown* (Heavy Dreadnought)
- **Background**: Academy prodigy promoted rapidly under wartime attrition. Stride is calculating, unflappable under fire, and an expert in deep-space firing solutions.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Coordinates multi-ship missile swarms and point-defense interception barriers.
  - Acts as the operational balance to Jaxen Vance's aggressive frontline instincts.

---

### Admiral Christopher Zhao — Supreme Commander, Sol Fleet
- **Role**: Strategic Fleet Commander
- **Headquarters**: Orbital Citadel *Olympus*, Luna Orbit
- **Background**: The stoic supreme commander tasked with humanity's survival. Balances political pressure from civilian planetary governors with the stark reality of dwindling naval reserves.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Master of grand interstellar logistics, orbital defensive grid deployment, and planetary fortification.
  - Must navigate ruthless moral choices—deciding which outer colony stations to sacrifice in order to preserve the core defense line around Earth.

---

### Chief Engineer Tarek Thorne — *Revenant* Propulsion Specialist
- **Role**: Technical Specialist
- **Background**: Former deep-space salvage engineer drafted into Sol Fleet service. Knows how to keep damaged fusion reactors running on sheer ingenuity, duct tape, and black-market parts.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Innovator of experimental reactor overdrive sequences that allow *Revenant* to evade alien tractor locks.
  - Cynical humor, perpetually coated in hydro-grease, fiercely protective of the ship's engine room.

---

### Xylar / The Shadow Herald — Incursion Swarm Overlord
- **Role**: Primary Antagonist / Extraterrestrial Commander
- **Faction**: The Shadow Incursion
- **Background**: The psionic vanguard consciousness guiding the bio-mechanical fleets pouring through the Kuiper Belt rift.
- **Attributes & Skills**:
  - Telepathic disruption capabilities capable of inducing panic and sensory hallucinations across human bridge crews.
  - Directs void-adapted bio-cruisers that harvest planetary biospheres and absorb starship hull materials to replenish swarm losses.
  - Views human civilization as an inefficient organic anomaly ripe for biological consolidation.
