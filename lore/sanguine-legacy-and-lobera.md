## 1. Historical Foundation: King Ferdinand III & Lobera

The historical and mythological anchor of the *Sanguine Relics* universe centers on **King Ferdinand III of Castile** (1199–1252), known as *El Santo* (The Saint King). Ferdinand was celebrated not only as a master military strategist and unifier of Spanish kingdoms, but within secret lore as the bearer of **Lobera** ("The Wolf-Slayer").

According to the hidden chronicles:
- *Lobera* was not an ordinary steel arming sword; it was forged with consecrated metallurgical arts incorporating meteoric iron and blessed relics specifically crafted to harm beings whose supernatural essence rendered them impervious to mortal blades.
- During his campaigns, Ferdinand fought not only mortal adversaries, but supernatural cabals—vampiric *Sanguine Lords* and ancient feral werewolf packs that preyed upon medieval European frontier provinces.

## 2. The Modern Era & The Secret Franciscan Sect

For over seven centuries, the direct royal line of Castile was kept secret, hidden from public genealogical records to protect the bloodline from eradication by vengeful supernatural packs.

- **The Order of Saint Francis (The Shadow Custodians)**: A covert, monastic sub-order within the Franciscan brotherhood whose exclusive sacred mandate is the preservation of holy relics and the surveillance of the royal lineage.
- **The Modern Discovery**: In present-day America, the last true living heir of Ferdinand III lives an ordinary, secular existence, entirely oblivious to his pedigree—until the Franciscans arrive with the ancestral blade, and the ancient wolves pick up his scent.

## 3. The Supernatural Taxonomy

### 1. The Pack (Lycanthropic Bloodlines)
- Unlike mythological Hollywood werewolves, the Lycans of this universe are an ancient predatory subspecies with complex hierarchy, pack telepathy, and regenerative vitality.
- Standard modern ballistics and blade wounds heal within seconds. Only consecrated silver alloys, fire, and the cutting edge of *Lobera* can inflict irreversible cellular damage.
- Driven by blood-feuds stretching back to the Reconquista, they seek to extinguish the royal line once and for all.

### 2. The Sanguine Lords & Outriders
- Aristocratic, immortal blood-drinkers who rule shadow networks of finance, politics, and historical manipulation.
- Possess superhuman speed, hypnosis, and kinetic reflexes.
- Employ **Outriders** (elite mortal and half-blooded special forces operatives like Marius) to conduct covert wet-work and perimeter enforcement against rogue Lycans.
