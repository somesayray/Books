## 1. Setting Overview & The Galactic Military

In an interstellar human diaspora where corporate cartels and frontier planetary rebellions threaten core systems, the **Galactic Military** represents the sole pillar of stability and the primary engine of socioeconomic mobility for youth across impoverished outer colonies.

To survive against lethal orbital skirmishes and alien bio-terrors, the Military established the **Sentinel Protocol**—an extreme, classified enhancement initiative producing augmented infantry known colloquially as **Chrome Soldiers**.

---

## 2. The Chrome Augmentation Process

Selection into the Sentinels is ruthless. Only a tiny fraction of candidates survive the psychological screening and physiological grafting:

1. **Sub-Dermal Chrome Lattice**: Nanomaterial weave bonded directly beneath the dermis, providing kinetic dissipation against ballistic impacts and environmental extremes.
2. **Neural Combat Telemetry (NCT)**: Direct cortical interface streaming situational awareness, target trajectory prediction, and squad telepathic data-links.
3. **Enhanced Myofibrillar Actuators**: Artificial muscle fibers boosting physical speed and leap capacity fourfold over baseline humans.

---

## 3. Reaper Squad & Orbital Drop Doctrine

**Reaper Squad** is the vanguard reconnaissance and shock assault cadre of the Sentinels.

- **Drop Pod Infiltration**: Squad members deploy from high orbit inside ablative single-occupant atmospheric drop capsules, punching through planetary ionospheres at hypersonic speeds.
- **Preston Marshal**: The anchor protagonist whose entry into Reaper Squad marks the transition of the Sentinel program from experimental prototype to battlefield dominance.
