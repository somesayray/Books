# Lore: Metroplex & The Spurloc Cyber-Noir Universe

## Overview
The **Metroplex** is a sprawling, neon-drenched coastal megacity of the late 21st century where bleeding-edge cybernetics, unchecked megacorporate dynasties, and hidden arcane warlock magic collide. It is a city defined by extreme verticality: towering glass-and-chrome corporate spires pierce smog-thick clouds for the billionaire oligarchs, while rain-soaked alleyways, black-market chop shops, and subterranean dive bars form the underworld of the Lower Boroughs.

---

## 1. Setting: The Metroplex

### Vertical Architecture & Social Stratification
- **The Spire District (The Apex)**: The stratosphere-level penthouses and corporate citadels owned by multi-trillion-dollar dynastic families like the Danforths. Monitored by private military contractors, combat drones, and hardline electronic firewalls.
- **Mid-Tier (The Grid)**: The bustling commercial arteries, neon-lit transit hubs, residential cube apartments, and city police precincts.
- **The Lower Boroughs (The Sinks / The Sub-Levels)**: Rain-drenched streets overflowing with unlicensed cybernetic chop shops, warlock apothecary parlors, synth-noodle stands, and illicit speakeasies. Here, the city municipal police rarely venture without tactical armored gunships.

### The Duality: Cybernetics and Arcane Warlock Magic
Unlike pure cyberpunk settings, Metroplex exists in a world where ancient warlock heritage runs quietly through mortal bloodlines alongside technological advancement:
- **Cybernetic Augmentation**: High-grade mil-spec prosthetics, neural coprocessors, optical HUD implants, and synthetic dermal weaves.
- **Warlock Lineages**: Inherent metaphysical bloodlines capable of channeling elemental energy, protective runes, sensory scrying, and warding glyphs.
- **The Hybrid Interface**: Rare individuals—most notably private investigator Martin Spurloc—interface occult warlock runes directly through cybernetic neural circuits, producing electrified sigils and bio-mechanical magic.

## 2. Key Organizations & Dynasties

### The Danforth Dynasty & Danforth Heavy Industries
- One of the ruling elite families of Metroplex.
- Led publicly by industrial titans and privately plagued by bitter inheritance infighting, corporate espionage, and illicit contracts.
- The patriarch **James Danforth III** represents the pinnacle of untouchable Metroplex wealth until his shocking and sensationalized murder.

### Metroplex Municipal Police Department (MMPD)
- Underfunded, politically shackled, and outgunned by corporate security forces.
- High casualties from domestic terrorist bombings and cartel shootouts.
- Relies heavily on cyber-reconstructed officers before discharging them when medical insurance or tactical utility expires.

### The Underworld Syndicate & The Art Syndicate
- Transnational crime rings operating smuggling channels between Metroplex, Neo-Chicago, and European freeports.
- Features legendary high-value operatives like **Hiro Yakatashi**, the world's most wanted art thief, pursued relentlessly by Interpol and corporate bounty hunters.

## 3. Notable Technologies & Entities

### "Elvis" — Autonomous Rogue AI
- An escaped, military-grade tactical and decryption artificial intelligence that slipped off a corporate black-site server.
- Adopted by Martin Spurloc into his local cyberdeck and trenchcoat communicator array.
- Self-configured with the flamboyant personality, cynical swagger, musical cadence, and snarky vernacular of an old-world rock-and-roll icon.
- Acts as Spurloc's forensic analyst, wiretapper, surveillance hacker, and eccentric moral compass.

### Cybernetic Prosthetics & Combat Augs
- Mil-spec left-arm kinetic chassis, internal biometric dampeners, and tactical optical HUDs that display real-time trajectory curves and occult resonance readouts.
