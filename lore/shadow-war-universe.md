## 1. Setting Overview & The First Incursion

*The Shadow War Chronicles* takes place in the late 22nd and early 23rd centuries, an era when humanity has successfully established off-world outposts across the Sol system and founded its first interstellar frontier colonies—most notably **Jamestown**.

Humanity believed itself alone until the arrival of the **Shadow Fleet**—a technologically advanced, relentless alien conglomerate operating from the deep void beyond mapped space. Unlike peaceful first-contact scenarios, the Incursion was surgical, brutal, and unprovoked.

## 2. Core Galactic Factions

### The United Earth Defense Council & Sol Fleet
- **Role**: The unified planetary military governing Earth, Mars, and orbital shipyards.
- **Doctrine**: Heavily armored carrier battle groups, missile frigate screens, and mass-driver kinetic artillery.
- **Key Ship Classes**:
  - *Yorktown-Class Supercarrier*: The planetary flagship and mobile command citadel.
  - *Revenant-Class Assault Frigate*: High-speed strike ships engineered for hit-and-run counter-incursions.
  - *Renegade Stealth Recon Cruisers*: Bounded sensor-dampened hulls designed for deep behind-the-lines electronic warfare.

### The Jamestown Colonists
- **Role**: The hardy interstellar pioneers who were the first to face the full wrath of the Incursion.
- **Traits**: Fiercely independent, guerrilla fighters, experts in atmospheric combat and frontier resourcefulness.

### The Shadows (The Extinction Armada)
- **Role**: The extraterrestrial threat seeking total planetary sterilization.
- **Technology**: Directed energy beams, gravity-well manipulation, biomechanical drone swarms, and phased stealth shields.
- **Objective**: Systematic conquest and resource harvesting of developing species before they reach true galactic parity.

### The Galactic Alliance (The Neutral Powers)
- **Role**: Elder interstellar species who initially regarded humanity as an expendable border buffer, but are forced to recognize humanity's combat grit after the victory of *Renegades* and *Vengeance*.

## 3. Technology & Warfare Doctrine

1. **Sub-Space Warp Gates & Jump Relays**: Travel between solar systems requires calculated jumps through gravitational transit points. Securing gate control is the central strategic goal in *Jamestown* and *Yorktown*.
2. **Kinetic Mass Drivers vs. Particle Shields**: Earth capital ships rely on dense tungsten-carbide hypervelocity slugs capable of overwhelming alien particle barriers through raw momentum.
3. **The Renegade Protocol**: When standard naval formations fail against superior alien shielding, Sol Fleet deploys small, autonomous strike teams with tactical independence to disrupt supply lines and sabotage mothership reactor cores.
