# Outline: Sanguine Relics & Lords Trilogy

## Series Concept
An urban and post-apocalyptic dark fantasy saga bridging historical Catholic mysteries, sacred blade relics, vampire covens, and feral werewolf wars. From modern American cities hiding medieval relics to the ruined post-fall seaboard ruled by autocratic vampire warlords, the saga charts humanity's struggle against nocturnal apex predators.

## Detailed Book Breakdown

### Book 1: *Legacy (Lobera)*
- **Logline**: When an unassuming modern American archivist inherits a 13th-century iron trunk, he unleashes *Lobera*—the legendary consecrated blade of Saint King Ferdinand III—and ignites a shadow war with ancient vampire covens.
- **Act I: The Inheritance**: Christian Vance receives a mysterious heirloom delivery following the sudden death of an estranged European relative. Opening the iron box, he touches the hilt of an ancient broadsword engraved with Latin verses. His blood awakens the weapon's dormant silver aura.
- **Act II: The Shadows Gather**: Within hours, hit squads of European vampires and rogue mercenaries assault Christian's home. He is rescued by Father Lorenzo, an operative of the Franciscan Order of San Fernando, who reveals Christian’s true genealogy as the direct descendant of Ferdinand of Castile.
- **Act III: The Cathedral Showdown**: Christian and Lorenzo retreat to a consecrated cathedral in Seville. Surrounded by an elder vampire coven seeking to melt down the blade that slaughtered their ancestors in the Reconquista, Christian masters the sword’s preternatural resonance and slays the elder vampire lord, securing his legacy.

---

### Book 2: *The Pack*
- **Logline**: The awakening of *Lobera* creates shockwaves across the preternatural world, breaking an uneasy truce between vampire bloodlines and the Appalachian werewolf packs.
- **Act I: Blood in the Mountains**: A surge of savage werewolf attacks along the East Coast threatens to expose the supernatural world to the human media. Christian Vance and Father Lorenzo travel into the deep wilderness to investigate whether a rogue vampire faction is staging false-flag atrocities.
- **Act II: The Lycan Civil War**: Christian encounters Asher and the traditional pack elders, who reveal a terrifying development: a younger, rabid pack faction has been corrupted by vampire blood-alchemy, turning into unstoppably violent berserkers.
- **Act III: The Battle of Black Ridge**: Christian wields *Lobera* alongside honorable werewolf warriors in a brutal clash against the corrupted pack and their shadowy vampire puppet masters, restoring balance to the forest before the secret leaks to the world.

---

### Book 3: *Outrider: Reign of the Sanguine Lords*
- **Logline**: In a dark future where civilization collapsed and vampire overlords rule feudal city-states, the betrayed commander of Lord Artemis's death squad rises from a mass grave in ruined Baltimore to lead an army of outcasts.
- **Act I: The Execution of Marius**: Marius, the greatest field commander in Lord Artemis’s vampire army, is ambushed, shot, and left for dead by his own lieutenants in the toxic ruins of derelict Baltimore because Artemis feared his rising popularity among the troops.
- **Act II: The Strays of Baltimore**: Marius awakens in a subterranean clinic run by a foul-mouthed, renegade human doctor. Stripped of his rank and honor, he discovers "The Strays"—an underground network of unenslaved mortal survivors, rogue half-bloods, and outcast lycanthropes struggling against Artemis's tyrannical blood tribute.
- **Act III: The Fall of the Sanguine Citadel**: Marius trains the Strays into a disciplined guerrilla strike force. Infiltrating Artemis's neon-lit skyscraper palace during the annual blood feast, Marius confronts his former master and executioners in a ferocious duel, dismantling the vampire dynasty and liberating Baltimore.
