## Editorial & Narrative Completion Summary

### 1. The Heritage of King Ferdinand III & Lobera
- Connects the historical prologue in 13th-century Castile/Austria with modern-day Maryland, Virginia, and the Scottish Highlands.
- Establishes the sacred celestial-iron blade *Lobera* and the Jamestown training dagger keyed to the royal hemoglobin sequence of King Ferdinand III.
- Fully realizes the author's foundational lore concept from `Werewolf novel.docx`: the discovery that Lycanthropy is not demonic or Satanic, but an ancient Pleistocene genetic defense adaptation that early humans evolved to survive prehistoric apex predators.

### 2. Character & Plot Arc Completion
- **Chapters 1-13 (Preserved & Normalized)**: Follows Victor Vance, an ordinary family man and park ranger, as a brutal animal attack in Great Falls draws the Franciscan Order (Bishop Talbot, Father Edgar, Sam) into his life. His house is attacked by Brock's rogue wolves, forcing Victor into exile to protect Jessica and his children. Through the Jamestown dagger, Victor absorbs ten years of combat muscle memory from Alfonso of Castile.
- **Chapters 14-22 + Epilogue (Authored & Completed)**:
  - *The Captain's Chair*: Introduces Captain Thomas Mercer and the extremist *Custodes Sanguinis* plotting mass genocide via weaponized aerosol silver ("Project Argentum").
  - *Highland Steel*: Edgar trains Victor in the Grampian foothills, unlocking the Aramaic command words (*Bakhzataa*, *Malka*, *Shlama*) and celestial kinetic resonance.
  - *Scent of the Pack & Night at Dundee Manor*: Brock and Martin lead a berserker assault on the Scottish sanctuary, critically injuring Edgar.
  - *Kinship of Blood & The Truth of the Genome*: Sowain reveals his true identity as Alfonso of Castile, the former 17th-century hunter who took the bite to preserve peace.
  - *The Breach at Glen Clova & Judgment of Lobera*: An unprecedented alliance of Hunter and Pack storms Mercer's staging ground at Saint Jude's Monastery, defeating Brock, neutralizing the aerosol rig, and ending Mercer's coup.
  - *The New Covenant & Guardian of the Shadows*: Bishop Talbot remands Mercer to Vatican custody. Victor establishes the New Covenant between humanity, the Order, and the Lycan Council. In Maine, Victor is reunited with his family, standing as the eternal Arbiter of the Relics.
