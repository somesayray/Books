## Editorial, Continuity & Proofreading Summary

### 1. Protagonist Name Consistency
- **Marcus Gavin**: Unified protagonist name across the entire book. In earlier drafts, paragraph 12 of the Prologue had used the placeholder surname 'Quentin' (`Captain Marcus Quentin`); this was harmonized to `Captain Marcus Gavin` to match all subsequent chapters, Book Two (*Black Queen*), and the master character bibles.

### 2. Spelling, Homophone & Typographical Corrections
- **The Four Tenets**: In the *Book of Aurtus* epigraph and dialogue, corrected 'four tenants' to 'four tenets'.
- **Reins vs. Reigns**: Corrected multiple instances of 'tied the reigns', 'handing the reigns', and 'pack animals reigns' to 'reins' when referring to horse riding gear.
- **Their vs. There**: Corrected over 25 homophone errors ('there low light' -> 'their low light', 'there white dresses' -> 'their white dresses', 'there backs' -> 'their backs', 'there will with thought' -> 'their will with thought').
- **Than vs. Then**: Corrected comparative homophones ('lower light then' -> 'lower light than', 'more then' -> 'more than', 'other then' -> 'other than').
- **General Erod Durass**: Corrected 'Duass' to 'Durass' and 'andUri' to 'and Uri'.
- **Vocabulary Polish**: Corrected 'handn't' -> 'hadn't', 'defeaned' -> 'deafened', 'explaimed' -> 'exclaimed', 'pressession' -> 'procession', 'iniates' -> 'initiates', 'Spector' -> 'Specter', 'lezurely' -> 'leisurely', 'emersed' -> 'immersed', 'here me praise' -> 'hear me praise', and 'letter of complement' -> 'letter of compliment'.

### 3. Spacing & Punctuation Cleanliness
- Removed 216 occurrences of leading spaces before commas (` ,` -> `,`).
- Fixed 12 occurrences of space before terminal punctuation (` .`, ` ?`, ` !`).
- Normalized spacing around smart quotation marks and dialogue tags.
- Separated run-together chapter headers such as `TWOOrah` into `Chapter Two: Orah`.

### 4. Trilogy Continuity Alignment
- **The Fall & Awakening**: Marcus Gavin arrives from the ARC cryo-pod, learns the new pagan-like religion of the *Book of Aurtus*, and bonds with Orah on her isolated mountain homestead.
- **The Empress's Incursion**: Rogan and the Empress's black-eyed altered soldiers arrive with new resin crystal-fire cannons, destroying the old balance of power and scattering the Wardens.
- **Bridge to Book Two (*Black Queen*)**: The climax neatly lines up the Wardens (Corliss, Uri, Yorst, Durass) retreating into the Barrens toward Castello, while Marcus and Texas-veteran Maelen escape into the wilds with the spectral wolf, directly setting the stage for *Black Queen*.
