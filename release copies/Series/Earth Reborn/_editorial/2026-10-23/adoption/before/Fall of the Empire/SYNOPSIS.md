# Fall of the Empire
## Intended Earth Reborn Book Three

**Author:** Raymond Bayly  
**Editorial status:** Distinct third novel required; current release manuscript duplicates earlier books.

## Current manuscript inventory

The current file contains a prologue, 22 numbered chapters, and Holy Ruler as an epilogue.

The Dawn of Subjugation and Northern Dominance repeat the final two chapters of The Fallen with small copyediting differences. The remaining 20 numbered chapters repeat The Black Queen, beginning with In Search Of and ending with Wardens' Keep. The epilogue repeats the same transfer of power and Marcus’s departure north.

This is not evidence of a second campaign following a failed assault in Book Two. The supplied texts tell substantially the same campaign twice.

## Intended narrative role

The author confirmed on 23 October 2026 that this must be a distinct third novel. A separate sequel synopsis cannot be certified from the present release file.

One possible revision assigns the final campaign to this title and removes it from Book Two. Another keeps Book Two intact and develops a new third narrative from the unresolved Empress/Rogan escape, the danger of Nero’s regency, and Marcus’s investigation of the threat across the waters. These are editorial alternatives, not established events.

See the series editorial report for the chapter comparison and a concrete reconstruction proposal. No reader-facing blurb is supplied until the actual Book Three story is settled.
