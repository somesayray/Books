# The Black Queen
## Earth Reborn Book Two

**Author:** Raymond Bayly  
**Current structure:** Prologue, 20 chapters, and epilogue

## Reader-facing description

The Empress’s rule leaves little room for mercy. Lord Seer Rogan hunts the man she wants while tightening his grip on the people already under his command.

Marcus Gavin needs allies—and a better understanding of the power that has kept him alive. Among the Silvans, he finds knowledge that may let him face the crown. Elsewhere, Nero gathers the dispossessed under the Church’s protection, carrying a hatred that grows with every atrocity he witnesses.

As resistance becomes war, Marcus must fight beside people whose vision of peace may leave no place for him.

## Editorial synopsis — spoilers

Rogan enforces the Empress’s demands through exemplary violence and interrogation. Marcus’s search for knowledge takes him into Silvan society, where runic transformation gives him capabilities beyond his earlier use of the gift.

The military conflict develops alongside a struggle over who has the right to govern. Corliss and the Wardens seek practical alliances. Nero’s work among refugees becomes increasingly militant, and the possibility of a Church regency acquires political force.

The current manuscript proceeds through the Check and Mate chapters into preparations for war, rescue efforts, siege fighting, and the confrontation at the capital. The Empress identifies herself as Eleneaor, a commander associated with the ancient conflict involving Tokimada and Zathris. Her warning of a threat across the waters complicates her motives without excusing her methods.

Marcus and Corliss defeat the constructs released during the confrontation, but the Empress and Rogan escape. In Holy Ruler, Nero announces the Church’s stewardship, Corliss leads the Wardens, and Marcus departs north to investigate what lies beyond the known conflict.
