## Editorial, Continuity & Proofreading Summary

### 1. Prologue Integration
- In the original working draft, the author had left a placeholder note: `Write the prologue covering the fallen stuff people need to know before comtinuing until he was in his possession.`
- Authored a comprehensive, canonical Prologue (*The Scars of the First Age*) bridging the cataclysmic events of *The Fallen*: the ARC stasis pods, the crystalline metamorphosis of Earth Reborn, the rise of the Gifted and the Church of Aurtus, the emergence of the Black Queen, the clash at the mountain outpost, and Marcus Gavin's perilous flight into the wild.

### 3. Spelling, Homophone & Typographical Corrections
- **Homophones & Possessives**: Corrected multiple occurrences of 'there' -> 'their' (their eyes, their heads, their weapons, their camp), 'who's' -> 'whose' (whose only reason, whose face), 'reigns' -> 'reins', and 'tenants' -> 'tenets'.
- **Vocabulary Polish**: Corrected 'nessescary' -> 'necessary', 'untill' -> 'until', 'a worst punishment' -> 'a worse punishment', and 'knocking the air of out him' -> 'knocking the air out of him'.
- **Punctuation Cleanliness**: Removed stray leading spaces before commas (` ,` -> `,`), periods (` .` -> `.`), and question marks. Normalized smart quotation marks.
- **Epilogue Header**: Corrected corrupted draft OCR string `Q 1Holy Ruler` to `Holy Ruler`.

### 4. Trilogy Continuity Alignment
- **Marcus Gavin**: Follows Marcus as he explores his Source powers, seeks allies among the Silvan folk, and evades the Black Queen's Chosen.
- **The Black Queen / Empress**: Her dominion deepens as Chancellor Juric Rogan executes brutal pacification campaigns and prepares for war against the neighboring realms.
- **The Wardens & The Church**: Chronicled through the eyes of Lorin Corliss and Messenger Veric Nero, detailing the rise of the fanatical *Sword of the Faithful* and the ultimate defense of the Wardens' Keep.
- **Bridge to Book Three**: Closes with the seeds of the final imperial war, directly leading into *Fall of the Empire (Draconis)*.
