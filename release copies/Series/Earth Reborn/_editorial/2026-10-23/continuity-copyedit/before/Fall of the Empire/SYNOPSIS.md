# Fall of the Empire
## Earth Reborn Book Three

**Author:** Raymond Bayly  
**Working structure:** Eight chapters and Holy Ruler epilogue  
**Status:** Adopted trilogy division, 23 October 2026

## Reader-facing description

Marcus Gavin has gained power enough to oppose the Empress. Now he must decide how to use it—and whom he can afford to trust.

As the armies gather, the Wardens, the Silvans, and the Church confront a common enemy with very different visions of the peace that should follow. For Marcus, preparations for war bring choices that cannot be settled by strength alone.

Defeating the Black Queen may save the people she threatens. It will not decide who rules them next.

## Editorial synopsis — spoilers

The volume opens with Marcus preparing runic weapons and armor as war approaches the City in the Trees. The need to defend the Silvans intersects with the effort to recover Maelen, forcing the allies to weigh personal loyalty against their wider obligations.

The campaign proceeds through rescue efforts, siege fighting, and the confrontation at the capital. The Empress identifies herself as Eleneaor and claims a history in the ancient struggle involving Tokimada and Zathris. Her warning about a threat across the waters complicates her motives without resolving the consequences of her rule.

Marcus and Corliss defeat the constructs released during the confrontation. The Empress and Rogan escape in the confusion.

In Holy Ruler, Nero announces the Church’s stewardship. Corliss takes command of the Wardens, while Marcus leaves northward to investigate the disturbing possibility raised by the Empress. Victory ends the immediate struggle for the capital but leaves the survivors facing an uncertain political future.

## Editorial boundary

These eight chapters were formerly chapters 13–20 of The Black Queen. They are now numbered 1–8 here, and the epilogue appears only in this volume. The earlier duplicated opening chapters and recap have been removed from this working manuscript.

Opening orientation, scene divisions, Queen-origin continuity, and a full line edit remain outstanding. The distinct structure is adopted; publication readiness is not implied.
