# Prologue: The Scars of the First Age

> *"The world did not die when the sky caught fire. It merely held its breath, waiting in the frozen dark until the stones themselves learned how to speak."*
> — *Book of Aurtus, The Second Chronicle*

The ARC had carried twenty thousand sleepers away from a dying Earth. When its machines judged the planet safe, the ship released its passengers in pods. Some returned long after the others, waking to a world they could scarcely recognize.

Captain Marcus Gavin had been an engineer. He had built irrigation systems in the deserts of Africa; his combat training belonged to a life he could barely reach. On this changed Earth, those memories offered little protection.

Orah gave him a home. For a time, he believed he could keep it.

Her death ended that hope. The power Marcus unleashed against her killers left him alive, but it could not restore what he had lost. With Maelen’s help, he began to learn what it meant to be a mage—and what the Empress demanded of those who possessed the gift.

Others were learning the cost of her rule. The Wardens were scattered. The Church was under attack. Even the mages could not agree whether to resist or submit. Nero had seen creatures wield magic against the helpless, and each fresh atrocity hardened his judgment of everyone who shared their power.

In the north, the survivors sought allies. In the occupied lands, Rogan enforced the Empress’s will.

Marcus had survived his return to Earth. Survival would no longer be enough.

# Chapter One: In Search Of

Lord Seer Rogan sat upon the throne of judgment, the deep purple robes of his office wrapped around his shoulders like armor. Four men knelt before him.

“You are charged with stealing the Lord Seer’s food and abandoning your posts. How do you plead?” Loric read from the parchment in his hand.

The men had taken food to their families when they should have been working the Empress’s fields. Rogan had imposed rationing to supply the troops. The commoners complained of starvation, but he would not tolerate theft. Their duty was to serve him, and through him, the Empress.

They should be grateful he allowed them to eat at all.

“Pathetic,” he said. “How dare you steal from me?”

None of the men met his eyes.

He stood from the throne taking a step down closer to these men. “But, you have stated that your families are too large for the small amount of food I give you, is this correct?” he asked the four men, his tone almost gentle and finally the men looked up and Rogan could see hope in their eyes. All four nodded. “Yes my lord, we all have multiple children, what you offer barely feeds the adults and our children cry from starvation, we did not mean any disrespect towards you lord, we only wish to take care of our children” the man on the left proclaimed and Rogan smiled at him. “I can understand this, Loric” he called to his assistant who nodded at him. “Have the children executed and display the bodies in the courtyard as an example of what happens when you steal from me” he said, the men erupted into screams of protest, the man who spoke his face twisted in anger launched himself at the Lord Seer. “Pathetic” he said as he raised his hand calling forth the power as the man stopped four feet from the Lord Seer, his body hanging in the air.

“I would kill you for your insolence, but I feel letting you live to be a worse punishment” he said and then with a flick of his hand sent the man crashing against the back stone wall, hard enough to knock him out, but not hard enough to kill him. The men were taken out as the soldiers were given their orders to kill the children, they too left the hall, leaving only Rogan and Loric left. “My Lord” he heard Loric call and with a sigh turned towards his assistant. “What is it Loric?” Rogan asked gently, but he could see the young man’s face, the pain and horror at ordering the death of the children.

He knew the young man was going to balk at this and he understood, so Rogan walked over and put his arm around the young man “It's important we quash all resistance Loric, this will ensure we have no more problems and that our commoners stay in line, allowing us to concentrate on what's important, we have entire species to save my young assistant. When this is done you will be celebrated for your strength and will to do what was necessary” he said gently to the young man. He smiled as he looked at the area, he used to dispense justice, this room defined his reign and what happened here would help write the story of his dedication to humanity and to saving it he thought his eyes shone like that of a true believer. These people were a means to an end, the Empress had spent years showing him the true purpose of the common people were to serve the crown and in doing so becoming the foundation of a great empire.

He understood this, Rogan lived it and he would ensure that his people would work to serve him and sometimes that meant that some would have to be made an example of, by showing them that he would punish any deviation from his orders without mercy, he would create a smooth running machine that would supply the armies and in doing so make him the envy of the chosen 12. Rogan left the room, the echo of a smile still on his face as he entered the private quarters. As he began to gently remove the purple robe, appreciating the way the soft fabric felt as he ran his hand over it, folding the robe and placing it in the closet a light knock came at the door.

With a sigh he closed the closet and turned “Come in” he said and Captain Postric entered the small chambers that Rogan now called his own. “Sir, the two prisoners are talking and we might have some information that you would be interested in” he stated standing at attention as he always did in Rogan’s presence, it was one of the reasons that Rogan enjoyed having the captain around, the man understood his place and showed respect to those above him. Walking down the narrow halls, past the heavy wooden doors of the dungeon area, that was one thing he could appreciate about Galen, the man had taken care to create the perfect place to house and torture those who displeased the crown. The Lord saw the two men, hanging naked in the center of the small cell, blood dripping from fresh wounds on their torsos. The Mage stirred, finally coming awake after a few more rough prods from the Captain. “Tell him what you told me” Postric repeated. Rogan could see the unfocused eyes, this man had been pushed to the edge, his mind almost broken he thought.

Rogan took another step forward “Who was this man?” he asked but the mage had passed out yet again, with a nod to postric the captain began to prod the prisoner again until he stirred “Tell the lord the name of this man” the captain yelled “Marcus my lord, the woman called him Marcus” he said.

Yes of course he would despise him he thought. Postric began to walk towards the prisoner but Rogan held up a hand, this one was his he thought. Summoning his power, he began to excert pressure on the man’s groin, slowly increasing it until the man began to scream and howl, his body squirming as he tried to get away from the pain. “Yes Messenger, I can do this all day, you will tell me what I want to know or this will continue” Rogan purred, enjoying the moment, watching the man, helpless in agony that only Rogan could stop, it was power at its truest form he thought still smiling as the man howled louder.

Eventually the Messenger told him everything, how he had met Marcus what they had talked about and where he had said he was from. Rogan left the dungeon and ascended back to the keep proper a victorious smile on his face as he entered his quarters once againt to pack. It took almost two hours to orgnanize the guards and prepare the convoy before Rogan stood there, giving his final orders to Loric. “Sir, I still think you should allow us to find this man, your needed here Postric stated, his tone barely hiding the annoyance at having the Lord Seer usurp his authority on this matter. Rogan simply smiled, he liked Postric but that would only get this man so far if he did not turn his attitude around, his authorty was only that which Rogan gave him, the man needed to be reminded of that one in a while, and this was the perfect time. “Captain Postric, I will take these men and hunt down the fugitive myself, this is not a man you could stop, in fact if he wanted to, he would simply kill you with a a thought” Rogan finished. “I am the only one here who will have the ability to bring him down and capture him so that we may transport him to the Empress, did you prepare the mage belt?” he asked. The Captain presenting the box to the lord Seer. Rogan had never had to wear one, of course until recently his powers wouldn’t have been a threat to anyone. But he had seen them work, it had the ability to ground out the power that a Mage gathered to themselves before sending it out as a spell or force of will.

He would incapacitate this Marcus and then once he put the belt on him, put him in the caged cart they were bringing, finally delivering him to the Empress personally. The rewards he would receive from his Empress would be the envy of the chosen he knew. Smiling he started out with the precession. “Where to first sir, Postric asked as he rode next to the lord Seer, Rogan smiling as he thought about his quarry and the rewards that were in store for him. “The farm, where he lived with the woman” he said. Closing his eyes Rogan reached out with his power, finding the two Screamers as they canvassed the woods, they had the ability to pickup power and though it had been quite a while since Marcus had been there, he had hoped the two of them still might find a trail that would tell them where he had gone. If not, then they would use traditional methods until they got close enough.

But once they did and once, the two screamers that served him picked up the trail of the Mage they would not stop until he was found. The group rode towards the remnants of the farm.

Rogan looked over the derelict building that stood stalwart and charred, it was an unremarkable piece of property he thought as he surveyed the dilapidated barn and animal pens, anything of value having been pilfered well before his taking over the territory. “What happened here Captain” he asked as Postric had his men survey the property looking for any clues that might have been left either as to the life he had lived or to his eventual evacuation of it. They had come up with nothing, nor had they been able to find anything more then a biline as to the death of six collectors the day the building burned. “I fear we may never know my lord” Postric said coming back after dismissing his men to their horses. “I am not sure what we do now as there is no indication where this Marcus went once, he left here” he finished knowing his words wouldn’t please the Lord Seer. Rogan simple smiled, his eyes closed, what postric didn’t know was that a faint trail had been found and the screamers were heading south looking for more.

The group headed south, finding only a few abandoned camps and no sign that they had come this way. Rogan stayed out for almost two weeks before returning unsuccessfully to his keep. Rogan

could not sleep nor could he eat, dammit he thought he had been so close to finding the man who the Empress had sought.

On the third day however, things changed. Rogan had been eating breakfast when the door burst open “What is the meaning of this” he said annoyed at the interruption as a man covered with the dirt of riding long distance, entered the room “Im sorry my Lord, a letter from the Empress” he said, bowing and handing Rogan a sealed parchment.

Rogan and his troops rode out quickly heading towards the south. “We will find somewhere to wait for my quarry, we ride for a day and then secure a farm or inn, from there we will send out scouts until he is spotted, no one will engage him, he is mine” Ordered Rogan as they rode. Closing his eyes, he passed his orders to the Screamers as they slunk through the woods nearby, Engage him, secure him, run him down, but don’t kill him he thought to the screamers and felt the frustration of being robbed of the murder of anyone, they were simple creatures who’s primal thoughts were easily understood he thought.


# Chapter Two: Going Home

Shielding his eyes from the bright sun, Nero surveyed the fields that stretched as far as the eye could see, it had been many years since he had traveled from Aurtus’s Keep, the headquarters of his order and the place he had begun his journey as an embassador of the Revered Emisarry. He had forgotten how beautiful this territory could be, he had been resident in the keep of his small village for too long he reflected as he smiled over the sun-bathed fields of wheat and cotton that stretched out towards the mountains in the distance.

Nero wasn’t worried about getting too lost, the mountains were still in front of him and they had been at his back all those years ago, he was headed in the correct direction at least he thought he was, mounting his horse he easily found the market road the farmers would use to head into town and sell there harvest. Following the road, he passed farm after farm the small complexes busy as people worked the fields and took care of the animals that would make them money and feed them for the winter. There was no more noble pursuit Nero thought smiling as he passed men herding cows into a field. When he had started his quest he had been worried and vexed that the land might be under threat, but having passed the last week, seeing people working as usual and the land still the breataking beauty he remembered, he had begun to forget the urgency and horror that he and the mage had witnessed causing them to report to there perspective Order’s. Korlin and he had split ways a week ago, the Mage heading towards the east to the Order that he served. While Nero continued south towards the range of mountains that stretched across his current horizon. He had been feeling more relieved the further he rode, smiling as he followed the farmers fence, knowing this would lead him to town, he came over a ridge and the smile died on his face.

Surveying the area in front of him, he could see the smoke pillars rising into the sky, squinting he saw the wall of the village a mile out, but something wasn’t right, the smoke pire’s were coming from within the wall he thought. No that couldn’t be right, maybe they are simply clear cutting he thought, speeding up the horse as he rode. This is deja vue he thought pushing his legs faster as he tried to get to the small village that like others he had seen, even recently stood burning. “What is she hoping to prove” he asked no one, anger replacing the worry the Empress had gone too far, her mages had gone too far he reflected finally making it to the gate of the village, stopping short he soon realized that there was no reason to have bothered. Looking at the courtyard of the village a scaffolding had been erected and the pile of bodies next to those still left hanging told the story, the troops seeing this village as uncontrollable had hung the entire residents. Nero pulled off the cloak he wore to combat the cold evenings covering the small body of the child who looked no more then four. What could a four year old have possibly done to threaten these men of war he thought, the tears streaming down his face. Nero let out a scream of rage as these faces were added to the others that now made his consciousness home. “How dare you” he screamed to the sky “Why wouldn’t you save this child, or this child, or this one” he said pointing out the small limp bodies strewn between those of the adults. “What could we have ever possibly done to earn the displeasure so severe that these children had to pay the price” screaming he moved around searching the sky, looking for any sign that he could be heard. The Empress had gone too far, she had gotten the mages to aid her. How many villages he wondered were out there just like this, that they had decided to make an example of. Decided the young and the innocent needed to die to fuel the cause they used to motivate themselves to these means. Whats ends could possible be worth this he wondered. Tears now freely streaming down his face.

Nero dropped to his knees, surrounded by the corpses of these people who did nothing to offend the gods, nothing to spurn them, these bodies who until recently were the people who filled the keep praying to Aurtus, to ask for good crops of healthy children. “Aurtus, I don’t understand how you can do this, these people who have done nothing to you, nor to the gods you serve. How can the Empress have such a hold, I truly understand bad things happen? But this is too much my Lord, this is too much” he prayed pleading to the man he had dedicated his life to serve.

But no answer came, no sign to show him the gods were listening or that Aurtus himself had heard his prayer, so he stayed there on his knees until his legs went numb. Getting up his legs almost locked in place he finally began the process of burying the people of this village, to ensure they would have a proper burial, with each strike of the shovel into the dirt his hatred of the Empress and her mages grew, the fire stoked with the price of the dead. Nero vowed at that moment to not stop until the woman was dead, until the mages that served her were all dead, this was not the first time that mages had sought control, the church had been there to stop them and since that time they had become complacent, they no longer fielded a standing army, no longer had the weapons or the knowledge to combat those with the gift. Nero resolved to change that to the gods as his witness they would not become complacent again.

It began to dawn on him that maybe the punishment for these people wasn’t about what they had done, but instead what the church had. If his church had not disbanded the army, become lazy and complacent they could have moved against the Empress, stopped her before these horrible things had happened. But as of now, the church was as powerless as that child that now lay dead and cold on the ground he thought.

“Is that what this is my Lord, punishment for us, for those who dedicated ourselves to lead and protect these people. Is this our fault?” he asked standing in the shallow hole, his clothes covered with dirt as le leaned on the old shovel and considered this. For close to an hour he stood there, eyes closed as he thought about all he had seen and experienced and knew, knew deep in his heart that his people, the leader of the flock of those who saught guidance from the Emisarry could have been the champions who prevented this instead of victims, helpless and running.

It took Nero most of the night and half the next day to bury the bodies of these villagers, but he did it, without stopping, without rest and when he was done, he stood over the lumps of dirt that now held the village he stood in and said the words blessing there souls and asking Aurtus to receive them. Once done he rested, eating what little food he had before setting off towards Aurtus’s keep. His mind full of the contemplative thoughts of the last day. He would talk to the Lord Regent, it was possible he could assign Nero to build the army back up, to again search the great library for the knowledge to train the soldiers and outfit them with the gear protecting them from the mages.

As the mountains grew larger the seed of hate that had nestled into his belly began to grow, the fertilizer of subjugation and death the fertilizer used so that by the time he reached the base of rock that held his orders keep, the seed had grown into a sturdy tree dripping with the blood of those slain at the hands of the Empress and her soldiers. Mages he thought Mages are the problem they give license to this travesty, they are the reason we find ourselves once again bathed in the blood of innocent people, farmers and tradesman who lived a simple existence so that the Empress may try to wrestle a little more power and a little more territory for her own hubris his mind continued. Climbing up the mountain trail towards the lights of the keep that loomed over the fields below he was so lost in his thoughts that he almost ran directly into a platoon of Empress’s soldiers camped near the base of the tall mountain. He quietly circled the encampment, using the rocks and brush as cover, from the look of the camp they had been there a while and did not look like they were going to be leaving any time soon he reflected as he saw the half completed wood buildings of bunk houses that had begun to be erected.

He carefully made his way up until he finally stood outside the large keep, watching as soldiers ran patrols around the property. The guards out front no longer wore the sword design of the Aurtus protectorate but instead had the golden lioness of the Empress’s guard. Nero knew the castle well and circled around the old stone building, finaly reaching near the rear of the structure he found what he was looking for, completely unguarded was the coal entrance, a portal added in later years to make it easier to bring coal and wood directly into the kitchen area of the old stone hearth, Nero smiled as he noticed that even the patrols seemed to ignore the door altogether.

Since the door actually opened into a storage area, only those who had to carry the wood into the kitchen, knew that the large storage shelf at the back would swing in and open into the side chamber in the large complexs kitchen. The initiates and young messeners used to joke calling it the “secret entrance” but now Nero thanked Aurtus that it had been so built. He waited until the next patrol passed and then sprinted to the building, once there he threw open the building, breathing a sigh of relief when the iron lever gave easily, no lock or bar blocking and securing it. Closing the door behind him, he left his way around the old store room, but at the back he could see the light through the planks in back of the storage shelves, it took him almost ten minutes to feel around and find the latch that aloud the shelf to swing and winced as it creaked lightly as he pushed against the shelf, swinging it inward and slipping into the kitchen.

At this hour the kitchen was empty as it was well beyond dinner and breakfast wouldn’t start for a good few hours. He had a small window to slip in and out before he would be stuck within the old keep for the day. Feeling the time pressure, he quickly moved out of the kitchen and made his way along the dark hall to the servant’s staircase of the castle. He knew the patrols would use the main stairs and patrol the main foyer, since the servants quarters and staircases were well within the building and only one entrance allowed the servants to enter the complex into the servants rooms the guards didn’t waste soldiers on guarding it directly, instead opting to keep a guard on the exterioir door and anyplace that the servants areas intersected with the main complex.

He made it up the stairs and down another hall, coming to the door that would lead him into the outer foyer of the Lord Regents Room. But he froze as he approached the door, outside sat a sleeping soldier. He stood there looking at the soldier, he didn’t know wether to flee, hide or continue and for a few moments stood there frozen in indecision. After a few moments Nero was embolded by the slow rising and lowering of the young soldier’s chest as he slept deeply. Carefully he approached, coming to stand next to the young soldier as he dreamd about his love, or some wonderful adventure. Nero slowly opened the door, stopping only one as the soldier shifted in his seat. The messenger almost bolted then but the noise would have surely woken the soldier. So, there he stood waiting until he settled down and began breathing deeply once again.

Finishing openin the door he stepped through, not quite closing it in case he had to leave quickly. The door opened into the receiving chamber for the Lord Regent the plush ornate carpet and wall decorations meant to show the status of the man you were about to meet. Nero could make out the high-quality mahogany receiving couches and tables as well as the marble top of the coffee table. Each end table had an ornate gold crystal lamp. He quietly walked across the room and slowly opened the door to the Lord Regents private chamber.

Entering the main quarters of Lord Alber Corinth, current Lord Regent of the Church of Wic Nero was nervous to be in the presence of Aurtus’s own emissary on earth. “Lord Corinth, My Lord are you here” he whispered. A groan came from the bed as Nero watched someone shift under the ornate red velvet covers. Walking over he came to find a sweating and pale Lord Corinth, barely conscious laying in the expensive bed. Something didn’t look right he thought and peeled back the blanket, underneath the Lord Regeant was still in his robes of office, but something didn’t look right and at first Nero nearly missed the wound in his stomack because of the deep red robes the man wore.

But then he saw it, the wound looks mortal and from the demeanor of his Lord Regent, the man did not have long to live. “My Lord” Nero hissed trying to get the man to wake even for a moment. To have him die after Nero had come all this way would be a miserable way to fail the self-imposed mission. Corinth opened his eyes, though they were not focused “Who, wo is there” the slurred voice came. They must have drugged him, or his loss of blood was affecting his mind. Nero wasn’t sure, he was not trained as doctor and the limited knowledge he had in first aid would be no help here. Mostly the young Messener just felt helpless as he tried to get the old man to focus. “Its Messenger Nero..” he began and then suddenly the eyes focused, Corinth looked directly at him, his eyes going wide “Nero, it is you, how did you get in here, nevermind Listen, the Empress has taken the keep, they hoped to keep me around to ensure that no one was suspicios, however I managed to get a letter out to my counterpart in Dever, we received a reply that basically said, there was nothing they could do, the soldiers found the reply and realizing what I had done, well one stabbed me” he said looking down at his robes, what little energy he had seemed to be fading as his eyes began to swimp out of focus. “He stabbed me” he said again as if unable to believe someone would do that. “No help, no help is coming” he continued and that realization seemed to seal the old man’s fate and he slumped unconscious. The Empress had killed the Lord Regent, had tried to take out the religion that supported this land. He didn’t know if dever would forward the request for assistance to Turen, to the main keep of Wic where the Emissary of Aurtus currently called home,

But he wouldn’t dare hope that Turen or Dever would lend there soldier to safe them, he knew the politics involved, there would be censure and arguments but no soldiers would enter Vicor because they were all along the border waiting for the Empress to invade. Everyone knew it was coming, only a matter of when he thought. He sat there for a long time wondering what to do finally he spotted Lord Corinth’s hand and there upon it was the ring of office, the ring that made him the authority of the church in Vicor. Nero knew he didn’t have long but paused as he thought about what to do, there were a lot og messengers older and more experienced, but the leadership of the religion was here, or had been here, if the Lord Regent was dead, then here was no reason to keep them around. Someone had to take control, had to become the head of the order lest the order be lost. With a deep sigh and a small prayer. Nero worked the ring off of the finger of the old man, during the process Corinth had awoken again, after a moment he realized what Nero was trying to do, he first tried to pull away but he was weak and Nero held him still as he continued to work off the ring, the man, not able to do anything simply lay there crying. He knew that if Nero was taking the ring, they he thought him dead. Finally, the large gold ring came free and Nero whispered a small blessing for the man before slipping the ring into his pocket and retreating back into the receiving room. He slipped past the still sleeping guard and down the hall. It took him only about five minutes to exit the keep, with no sign of the patrol he rushed across the open ground making the brush before he heard the heavy footfalls signaling the approach of the sguard patrol.

He sped away from the keep, making his way north to rendezvous with Korlin it is possible that he may have had better success finding help within the mages order. He smiled wryly as he thought of the irony of Mages helping get rid of other mages, hell he wasn’t convinced that the mages who served the Empress weren’t with the order he thought. Was it possible that the order was in on it, that the Empress had made a deal with the once secretive leaders of the Mages order he wondered? Magic in the land had become scarce and those with it were nowhere near as powerful as in the past, but the stories he had heard of the ones serving the Empress were like the mages of old. They had power and used it easily to demonstrate their superiority, was this the inevitable outcome of the church leaving the mages alive, had the founders of the order who had hunted them down been right he wondered.

They two men lay stock still on the ground with the man not more then thirty feet from there location. If they were seen, they were dead Nero understood this as sweat broke out over his body.

The soldier finished his business and casually walked back into the building, laughter echoing out the door only to silence as it closed. The Messenger sighed, laying his head in the dirt and saying a short prayer. They both got up and finished rushing to the front of the old Keep.

The door was unlocked, and they went in and closed it behind them. Nero turned and did a doubletake, the keep was full, though not of people or things but supplies, boxes and bags of food and other sundries stacked from floor to ceiling stood in their way. Only a narrow aisle aloud any navigation to the front of the holy place. “What is this?” Gilread asked looking at the mountain of supplies that surrounded the two of them. “It’s the reason there are guards Gilread, this village is now a storehouse, though I am not sure why” he answered squeezing his way through the small space and coming to stand next to the podium. Since it had been carved from a solid piece of wood and extended into the foundation of the building it was next to impossible to move, so the soldiers had left the front area clear of boxes and bags.

“Help me Gilread” Nero said, as he tried to pry back a board behind the podium, the man looked at him curiosly and then crouched down to help. The board would not budge no matter how they pulled. “Give me a minute” Gilread said, moving back into the supplies and disappearing from sight. He returned a moment later with a smile and what looked like a flat piece of metal. “They left the pry bar” he said triumphiantly. Using the bar, they were able to peel back the boards giving them a four-foot opening. Peering into it Nero couldn’t see through the blackness in the hole and the two went searching again, this time finding one of the crystal lamps that had been thrown into a corner when they had cleaned out the old keep.

The glass was broken but after a moment of fiddling with the turn knob Nero was able to get a weak light and went over to the hold. Lowering the latern into it, he was rewarded with an ornate wooden lid of a coffin. Gilread looked confused “What?” he asked not understanding. Nero smiled “They didn’t put him in the ground, instead putting him under the floor should his sins come out and he need to be moved from this place. I never understood the decision, but sometimes those in charge don’t think through the concequences, I know for months the Messenger of the keep had to host services outside because of the smell, ever request to move the body was denied despite the pleas” Nero said grabbing the pry bar and putting it down into the hole. It took him a few attempts the area was tight and it didn’t give him a lot of leverage.

Finally, he was able to slide it between the lid and the cask, after a few pushes the lid popped. Pulling back the lid Nero shined the light into the coffin coming face to face with the mummified face of the old Lord Regent dressed in his finest robes. Wincing he reached his hand into the coffin and began feeling around, it took nearly ten minutes before his hand closed over a velvet bag with something heavy inside. With a smile he slowly pulled up the bag and pulled out the heavy silver stamp that was the seal of the Lord Regent. “Ive got it” he whispered, and he haphazardly replaced the boards.

Noticing the side room Nero made his way in to find the storage drawers used by the Messengers. Most of them were empty but in the final drawer he was rewarded with a few pages of old parchment and a small block of wax. He gently folded them and put them into his robes. Victorious, the two made their way back along the narrow space and had gone halfway through when Nero heard the door open. “Go back” he hissed to Gilread as the two began to shimmy back towards the pulpit. They disappeared around the edges of the mountain of supplies as Nero held his breath, the sound of slow booted footfalls echoing through the building. He heard the murmur of a voice from outside, but the one that answered was much closer. “I thought I heard something, give me a minute” the male voice shot back to whomever had spoken to him outside. Nero closed his eyes begging Aurtus to get away without being captured as the boots continued to move around the other side of the supplies.

He looked over to the other side of the small aisle and there was Gilread the man’s face was a mask of panic, Nero smiled at him making a calming gesture but ashis hand came down, the edge of his robe caught the prybar that had been laid on a supply box and it fell, hitting the floor with a loud thud. Both men winced and Nero heard the boots stop, standing still as the Soldier tried to narrow the location of the sound. After a moment he could hear the soldier moving again, but the pattern had changed and with dawning horror he realized the soldier was coming down the passage. “With a motion to Gilread he pushed himself up against the boxes trying to lessen the profile he would give off. Reaching into his pocked, his hand closed around the heavy seal and he pulled it out, raising it above his head he waited, listening as the sounds of shuffling came ever closer.

Then as he watched a hand appeared picking up the pry bar, holding it right in front of Nero the soldier grunted and as the two men listened the shuffling sound began to fade. After a few moments they listened as the boots crossed the open area and the door closed. Nero let out a breath that deflated his soul as he sagged with relief.

The two men shuffled through the supplies and stood waiting by the door as Nero fought his urge to open it and run. Slowly cracking it, he looked out and could see the light still in the smithy and he motioned Gilread, the two men exited the keep and ran as fast as they could to the barn. They reached it only a few minutes later, both men collapsing as their lungs burnt in their chest.

Once they had recovered, they made haste pushing the horses as fast as they could to make it back to the camp before dawn. Riding into the camp they were aloud back in by two disapproving guards after a lengthy explanation. Nero quickly made it back to the shack and sitting down scrawled a letter to the Lord Regent of Turen, heating the wax he pressed the hard-won seal against the folded paper. “Is it done?” came Gilread’s voice from the door and Nero turned and smiled. “Yes, now we just need to get it there, Gilread, I need to find a messenger someone to take this letter and put it in the hands of the Lord Regent of Turin, can you find me a volunteer, understand the future of Vicor may very well ride on the delivery of this letter” he said and the other man gave him a solemn nod leaving Nero to his thoughts.

An hour later a young lanky man stood in the small shack looking around nervously as Nero entered. “Master Gilread tells me you can ride young man, is this true?” he asked and the lanky man nodded “Yes Messenger, I ran the mail route between turen and a few of our smaller cities until… well until they closed the border Messenger” he answered and Nero smiled. “I need this letter delivered son, it is imperitave it gets to the Lord Regeant in Turen, do you undersand” he asked and the lanky man nodded, his eyes serious “I will make it so Messenger” he said and Nero reguarded him for a moment before nodding. “See Gilread, he will procure you a horse, once you have delivered the letter, wait for his response, and I hope his response includes troops” he said not daring to hope.

With the hopes of the camp and the future of there country on his shoulders the young man bolted forth riding the horse out and to the east for all he was worth. Nero entered his shack only to find an agitated Vira pacing the small floor, he was surprised to find her there “Vira, I know I should have told you of my trip but” he began but the woman cut him off. “That’s not it Nero, we have a problem, War is coming” she said and Nero nodded. He had been fielding that same worry from everyone. “I have just sent a message to Turen for assistance with troops to help us move somewhere out of the way, that doesn’t put us in the path of the enemy” he said smiling. But Vira did not share his smile instead the blonde capable women shot him a helpless look.

“No Nero, they are coming, now if we are here when they arrive, we will either be taken back as prisoners or slaughtered as examples” she said and nero’s stomach dropped about ten feet. “They are marching now. How do you know?” he demanded and Vira nodded “One of the Wardens who runs the patrol route south, brought us the news Nero, they will be here within a week” she finished. Walking over to his desk Nero slumped down into the chair “There is no way our support will be here in time, this came, all of the camps will be massacred” he whispered the vision of people swinging from poles as Ravens picked out their eyes flashed through his head.

“What do we do Vira, we can’t stay here” he said locking eyes with the woman who simply nodded but he could see the fear in her eyes as well. They were in the path of the Empress’s war machine and they would not stop to worry about them, they would roll over this camp without even a thought. “We need to find someplace safe Nero, we need to get these people out of here, we cannot wait, these aren’t soldiers these are farmers who have lost everything not one is able bodied enough to stand against the forces bearing down on us” she stated. Nero knew this, understood this and yet finding a solution was buried under the self pity and doubt that swam through his mind. He was no general, no leader and yet now the fate of all of these people rested on his shoulders, how was he suppoed to save them when he couldn’t even save himself.

“Nero” Vira said, her voice even and firm, looking up he saw the resolve in her eyes, they were fierce and determined “We will find a way to save these people, but I need you to help” she said her eyes never leaving his. The fierce look of determination burning away his self doubt as his mind began to turn. “I need maps, Vira, can you find maps of the area” he asked bolting upright and the woman smiled, reaching into her pack and pulling out a stack of parchment “right here Messenger” she said smiling.

They spent the evening and well into the night pouring over the maps looking at every inch of every curve of ink that covered the old parchment hoping to divine some solution or some place they could glean from the information in front of them. But they were not scouting nor were they familiar with the areas shown. To them it was just lines and notes none of them giving a hint to a location that would squester his people from the oncoming storm. They gave up for the night agreeing to meet back the next day. Nero did not sleep that night instead lying awake as scene after scene of people dieing by the swords of the people who once protected them flashed through his mind.

In these scenes he failed to protect those who trusted him, those who put faith in his ability to keep them from harm. Finally, as the sun began to peak over the horizon, he mercifully left his bed, eyes bloodshot from his restless night he splashed water on his face and prepared a light breakfast. Vira did not show up until two hours later, by that time Nero had found himself pouring once again over the maps and seeing nothing that would help him. A knock came at the flimsy excuse for a door and looking up he found Vira an excited smile on her face as she led a grizzled looking man into the shack. “Messenger” the said nodding his head in deference, a religious man Nero thought nodding to him and turning to Vira. “Nero, we have it” she said excitedly, and he held his breath as she motioned for the woodsman to continue.

The man was filthy as if he had been working all night, his blonde hair was ratty with leaves and twigs haphazardly sticking out of it. His clothes were half covered with mud not all of it dry. “Who is this Vira?” Nero asked and the man stepped forward clearing his throat. “I am goric Messenger, a Master Warden” he simply said, and Nero nodded as a kernel of hope sprouted in his mind if anyone knew the area it would be the wardens he reflected, leaning back and motioning for the man to continue. Goric looked between the two of them shifting uncomfortably as he stood there for a moment trying to decide where to start. “Messenger, many years ago before the Wardens were given the keep, we housed ourselves in an old Lords villa to the west of here, about five days walk. I am not sure of the current state of the property, but with an army coming it will be out of the way and possibly defendable should the Empress’s soldiers decide you are a worthy target” he said and then waited as Vira and Nero shot meaningful gazes at each other. “This is good information Goric, I thank you, Aurtus thanks you, you may have helped save these people, but I will need one more favor from you” Nero said gently and Goric nodded “What is it Messenger?” he asked.

A few hours later Goric had come through as a line of horses and a few carts of supplies lined up outside of the camps. Inside the four camps of Refugees it was pure chaos as everyone packed frantically. The Silvan’s though happy about the exodus of the Refugees, were not happy about the loss of supplies. Goric had convinced someone that for the low low price of the supplies their refugee problem would go away. It took almost four hours before everyone had mustered and lined up, the wounded were put in carts along with the old and the young. Vira’s female shooters took positions at the front and back of the caravan, at the very front rode Nero, Vira and Goric. Nero having requested the Warden as a guide to the location.

“Okay let’s get started” Nero said a smile on his face as for the first time in days, there was a real chance these people would be out of harms way. The caravan rode west slowly as the worn trade road was mostly unused and not easily traversed. The wagons shifted dangerously from side to side navigating water ruts forcing the group to keep to almost a walking pace. Nero had to keep himself calm and force down the frustration of the lack of progress five hours into the trip.

The caravan rode through the night Nero hearing a clock in his head, knowing the soldiers were on the way and needing to put distance between the border and his people tried his best to force more speed out of them, but to no avail. “So, I have to ask, what about the soldiers from Turen you requested” Vira asked on the third day as they continued to ride after an hour break for food. Nero smiled “I left them a note with the Silvan’s in case they showed up. Goric also left word with his people.” Nero finished, rubbing the ring that still felt new on his finger.

“What are we going to do when this is all over Nero, how are we going to rebuild. I image the Silvan’s; the Order and the Wardens are not going to stand by and allow the Empress her reign of terror” Vira proffered and Nero through about it for a moment. “There is something in place should we find ourselves without a leader, it called the Royal Stewardship, a clause put into the Messengers that if needed the Church would steward the territory until a suitable ruler was found. It was added generations ago to ensure that should a ruler die without an heir; the country wouldn’t descend into bloodshed” he finished and Vira looked at him questioningly. “Why is it I have never heard of this?” she asked as if she were one who would know and Nero shot her a glance he had never asked the woman what she had done prior to being a refugee as he was considering how to do that he shrugged “It has never been enacted before” he answered and Vira nodded. Nero continued to rub the ring that now weighed even more heavy upon his finger as he thought about the possibility of securing the country.

The rest of the journey was slow but uneventful and finally as they came around a corner on the old trade road a large grey stone wall rose in the distance. Goric smiled and pointed forward “Messenger, I give you Warden’s Hall” he said, and a cheer rose up from the line as the word spread, they were home. It took nearly a half of a day to finally reach the old wooden gate of the hall, Nero eyeing the rotten wood and the rusted hinges, the look of the wall was solid but everything else on it was either gone, rusted or broken which did not bode well for the rest of it he thought.

A small group went in first entering the overgrown courtyard as they surveyed the various buildings and the main hall, trees grew out of buildings and roofs had caved in long ago he noticed, but these things could be repaired and would give people something to do rather then wallow in fear of the Soldiers. He tasked Gilread to round up groups of able-bodied men and began the process of repairing a few of the surrounding buildings, they needed shelter and soon. Vira took charge of storing the carts within the wall as Goric himself went out to gather much needed wood to repair the door. Which left Nero wandering the complex, most of the people were cleaning up, chopping down or assisting in repairs.

Entering the large old hall swaths of sunlight lit the old worn stone floors as the roofs had caved in long ago, a tree now grew in the center of the cavernous hall reaching into the sky as a bed of rotten leaves covered the ground like carpet. He could see rotten abandoned pieces of what furniture at one time had been strewn about the area, but other then that the walls looked solid and the roof could be repaired. This would work he thought to himself, they could be safe here, weather the coming fight out of the way where the soldiers had no reason to go, he thought.

By the third day a few of the buildings had roofs and doors and people had begun to chip in more and more as they got settled Nero smiled as he watched repairs being done on all of the buildings left. The wall was solid and Goric had already begun to build a new solid door for the opening that was left when they removed the rotting wood that had once been the original protection for this place. As he strolled the compound nodding and offering words of encouragement, he could smell cooking meat from a tent setup at the rear where a line of people smiled and talked as they waited to eat. Things were going to be okay he thought looking around smiling “How are you today Messenger?” Vira’s voice came from behind him and Nero turned smiling and nodding at the woman. “Good, just inspecting the work and liking the progress, you know Vira for the first time in a while, I honestly believe we are going to be okay” he said smiling at her. She returned it nodding towards the wall. “My girls have been scouting out and tell me nothing at all is in our vicinity, we seem to be well out of the way here, so I think you might be right” she answered.

They lined up with the rest and ate lunch, talking about the work and the progress that had been made. After lunch Nero entered the main hall again, grabbing an axe he set his robe aside and began to remove the large tree in the center of the hall. Chop after chop he worked through the large trunk pieces of it flying as perspiration ran from his brow. It took him most of the day, ensuring the cut was true and that the angle would allow the tree to fall in the direction he wished, but by the time the sun fell to the trees there came a loud crack and the tree fell into the center of the large hall with a resounding crash. Nero smiled as he looked at the fruit of his labor, people crowded the door to see the downed tree. “Goric, do we have any woodsmiths with us, I believe I have found a new altar for the keep” he said, and a cheer rang out from the crowd and Nero laughed, something he had not done in many months he reflected.

But the haven was not as safe as he had thought, late that night as he slept soundly the dreams and visions of the villages he had seen and the atrocities he had been witness to faded a little, allowing him to sleep through the night an alarm went up through the camp and people scrambled out of bed and began to dress.

Nero burst out of the building that held forty people tightly until the rest of the facilities were completed and as he stepped into the open air a bone chilling shriek rose from the woods, a shriek he knew and one that sent a chill down his spine. “No, no Aurtus, not here” he whispered, looking around Nero did not know what to do, but thankfully Goric and Vira had appeared marshalling people to grab whatever they could find as a weapon they spread out over the courtyard as more of the shrieks rose from the woods, first three then five and then ten answered the original call. “It’s the demons, it’s the Lord Seers minions, they have found us” someone yelled from the courtyard, but Nero could not see them.

He looked around at the sixty people now scattered throughout the yard everyone’s head on a swivel as the screams echoed over the wall. The full moon gave them the ability to at least see, but see what he thought fighting the panic that began to well up inside. Then something ran along the wall, descending down again before he could get a look at it. It ran on all fours with the speed of a cat as it dashed. Screams echoed from somewhere near the back of the gate, not screams of fear no these were screams he had heard before, they were of pain and terror. Without so much as a thought for his own safety Nero dashed towards the sounds, people gathered as some tried to swing at whatever it was in the center of that crowd. But no sooner had a few tried then they were dragged down as well.

Nero burst through the crowd finding himself staring at the back wall and half open port gate as something hovered over a body its eyes glowed with blue hate as the creature hissed at the crowd. Then with the wave of its claw an unseen force hit the crowd driving them back as it focused back on its victim, Nero couldn’t tell who it was, the mask of torn meat and blood hit the unfortunate souls identity, but these things were mages he thought with horror.

They were too exposed, too out in the open, they needed to find someplace more secure he thought and then spotted the main hall, no it didn’t have a roof, but it did have walls. “Everyone, to the main hall” he screamed at them and then began shoving the shocked onlookers until they began to move.

How did they find them he wondered, why would they have been sent to kill them, they were nobodies, they had no military or strategic significance, the only thing they had done was to flee he thought? Then it dawned on him that the Empress might want to make an example of those who had escaped her, might want to show those still under her thumb that there was no hope. But in order to do that, they all would have to die. Nero pushed people harder, moving towards the keep as more shrieks rose from around them. Looking to his left he watched something rise up dragging down a poor girl who had found herself on the outside of the running crowd. To his right was another scream, but it quickly cut off and Nero didn’t want to think about the reason why.

The creatures were around them, picking off people as they desperately tried to make it to the main hall. A blast of fire hit to his left, igniting four people and killing them almost instantly. They were mages, or mage creations he thought. That same burning hatred he had developed since this had begun rekindled in force. This was the fault of those with the gift, those who the church had let live. This was the repayment for that mercy he thought hatred burning at his soul as he moved. More blasts of fire erupted around him as the creatures rained down terror on the moving mob.

Then he was through the door, people pushing further back into the hall, trying to get away from the entrance as more screams erupted from behind. As the last of the survivors entered, Nero slammed the newly installed door, grabbing the bar and pushing it into place, he no sooner had done that when something slammed against the wood sending mortar spilling to the ground from around the frame.

Nero backed away from the entrance, watching it as something again slammed into it, sending more mortar to the floor.

Then a scream came from above and he looked up to see one of the creatures, perched on the edge of the torn and fallen roof. Before he could move it leaped from its perch, coming to land directly in front of Nero. He stared at the creature in horror, its corpse white skin and long limbs snaked out as it hissed at Nero, reaching for him. At that moment all sanity left the Messenger as the anger and hate that had been festering in his soul for months finally boiled over. With a loud scream Nero flung himself at the demon that now stood before them, wrapping his hands around its neck as they both fell to the ground. He slid his hands up the creatures face “No more, no more” he whispered over and over as his thumbs found its eyes and pushed with all his strength, he felt the small warm orbs resist the tips of his thumbs for only a moment before the warm black blood spilled around the socket and flowed to the floor from around his hands as he continued to push, harder and harder, feeling the bone at the back of the socket, he pushed even harder. The creature thrashed and bucked underneath him. He didn’t even feel the claws as they sliced through his robes and bit into his skin, wet blood pouring down onto the thing underneath him.

Wrapping his hands around its head, he began to pick it up and slam it back down over and over the creature mewling as it desperately tried to throw the Messenger off. Finally Nero heard a satisfying crack as the demon finally stopped moving, finally going still beneath him and he smiled a malevolent smile, they could be killed, they could be hurt he thought and tilted his head up to the sky, thrusting the black blooded fingers as if clawing at the moon and he screamed and screamed. As he did the creatures eight of them now perched above, slid away into the night.
