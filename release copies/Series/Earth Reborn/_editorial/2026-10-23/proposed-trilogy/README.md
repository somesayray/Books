# Proposed Earth Reborn trilogy division

> **Adopted on 23 October 2026.** These candidates are retained as the approved structural reference. The book-folder Markdown manuscripts now use this division. Remaining editorial work described below still applies.

These are review candidates assembled from the refined Markdown text. They are not approved release masters and do not represent a newly written sequel. Original release files remain in their book folders.

## Proposed boundaries

- **The Fallen:** retain its existing prologue and all 16 chapters.
- **The Black Queen:** retain its refined prologue and chapters 1–12, ending with Check and Mate (Part III).
- **Fall of the Empire:** transfer Book Two chapters 13–20 into chapters 1–8, starting with Si Vis Pacem, Para Bellum; retain Holy Ruler as the final epilogue. Use Book Three’s existing title and publication front matter. Omit its contradictory recap and all repeated earlier chapters.

Every numbered chapter from the revised Book Two appears exactly once across the candidate Books Two and Three, without changing its body text. Book One is retained intact. Chapter counts and exact-text partition checks passed.

## What this resolves

The three files no longer repeat entire chapters across volumes. The final campaign, confrontation with the Empress, and regency belong only to Book Three. The sequence preserves the existing narrative order and requires no invented future events.

## What still needs editorial work

Book Two now ends with Nero killing the creature at the refugees’ refuge. That is a forceful scene ending, but it does not provide a closing beat for Marcus’s viewpoint. A brief final scene or revised ordering should establish what Marcus has chosen and the cost of his transformation before the volume ends.

Book Three opens with Marcus preparing his runic equipment. It needs a concise orientation pass for readers returning after a gap, and shorter scene divisions through the long campaign chapters. Existing standalone draft labels such as “Sic Pac Parabellum” and “Forced in Either” remain visible for review; these candidate files are intentionally not presented as finished copy.

The approximate balance is 151,000 / 62,000 / 50,000 words. Book One is much longer. Do not pad the later books to equalize them; assess scene purpose and natural breaks first.

These candidates do not reconcile Mortis’s Arianna with Victoria/Eleneaor, settle other canon conflicts, or complete a full sentence-by-sentence copyedit.

## Alternative

Keep the current Book Two intact and commission a genuinely new Book Three following the Empress’s escape, the regency, and Marcus’s northern journey. No such distinct continuation was established in the inspected Draconis source.

## Decision

Adopt this division for further development, or retain Book Two intact and develop a new sequel. Neither decision should be implied merely by the existing filenames.
