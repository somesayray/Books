# Chapter One: Si Vis Pacem, Para Bellum


Furnace light filled the workshop. Marcus stood over the craftsman’s table, listening to the rhythm of the smiths’ hammers as they worked metal, crystal resin, and carbon into long, slender blades. Recipes unused since the Mage Wars were taking shape again, following designs he had drawn from memories that were not his own.

Nearby, leatherworkers stitched silver and crystal into the armor. Beneath Marcus’s skin, blue runes pulsed with the flow of power. The workers told stories of ancient runic mages who could fly and throw buildings. He smiled at the tales, unsure how much to believe.

His own experiments had already exceeded anything he had imagined. Now he understood how the Seer, using an imperfect version of the same process, had so easily overpowered him. The Silvans had made Marcus feel damn near invincible.

“Master Mage, your blades are complete.”

He turned toward the tall, stout master smith. Her white hair was tied back in a tight ponytail. Durass had sent two of his finest smiths, and their work had justified his confidence.

She placed the blades in Marcus’s outstretched hands. He admired them for a moment, then carried them to the table where the other components lay.

“Master Mage, I noticed the unusual layering of runes,” she said. “May I ask what they do?”

“It’s a technique few have used,” Marcus replied. “The spells for defense and offense are interwoven so that they feed off one another. The first people used it.”

He left the rest unsaid. Inscribing the pattern required more than knowing its shape. He could see power flowing through the runes, a gift that let him understand their relationships as he worked.

“He is being modest, its much more than that, those spells are beyond even the knowledge of our rune masters” came a voice from the entrance to the room that now held the stations needed to build the weapons and armor the Mage needed. Marcus turned towards the door to see General Durass leaning against it smiling as he looked over the table at the pieces laying there. “I take it, they have given you everything you need?” he asked. Durass had been there when Marcus had drawn up the plans, he had reviewed them with him excited and in awe at the engineering behind them though Marcus could not take any credit for it, the plans were part of the gift the ancient Silvan Mage had given him. “They have, I was about to start scribing them, would you like to stay and watch?” Marcus ask knowing the man would decline, watching someone inscribe objects unless you could watch the power evolve and flow was tant amount to watching paint dry.

As suspected Durass declined with a shake of his head. “However, before you do, would you join me for lunch?” he asked and something about the request made Marcus narrow his eyes and take a good look at the man, there was worry in his eyes. He has news and I am not going to like it Marcus thought nodding his head accepting the offer.

Leaving the room as the Smiths and Crafters continued their work the two crossed the hall and entered a small private room that had already been setup with a table and chairs. The room, like the rest of the underground complex used by the Silvan Mages was completely made of crystal giving an erie feeling of seeing yourself over and over again while looking at a wall, similar to the effect of laying mirrors in front and behind you. It was enough to give a person vertigo Marcus thought taking a seat in one of the padded dark wood chairs that had been provided.

It was only a moment before waiters appeared carrying various trays of food and before Marcus knew it a vast spread appeared before them. Durass motioned for him to dig in and he quickly obliged the General. He had not realized just how hungry he was until that moment and he began carving off pieces of the large roasted bird and scooping heaps of a steamed yellow vegetable onto his place. “So, you didn’t just come down to take me on a date I take it,” Marcus said illiciting a small smile from the general, but one that quickly disappeared.

“No, I wanted to update you there are things in motion you need to know about, also there has been an incident,” he said, and Marcus watched as the man winced as the words left his mouth. “What sort of incident?” he asked leaning in the food temporarily forgotten. Durass looked away studying the wall for a moment and then with a sigh he spoke. “LowLanders were attacked by screamers, they seemed to know where they moved to and once they were settled, a small number of them attacked, some people died but most are okay,” he said and Marcus’s eyes narrowed “weren’t they in camps protected by your people,” he asked and Durass shook his head. “No, we knew they would be in the path of an army and the leader a holy man, came up with a solution to move them out of danger, taking up in an old Warden complex, they headed over a week ago and we just got word of the attack, apparently from what I can ascertain of the motives, they were to be made an example of, but we are not sure” the General said.

Marcus’s eyes narrowed “But that’s not all of it is it?” he asked knowing the information was bad, but the General wouldn’t have invited him to lunch, unless there was more. Durass sighed shaking his head “please understand my people are scared, they are not themselves..” he started and then stopped, shaking his head and then started again “The council will meet in the morning, they are going to vote on whether to exile the lowlanders, the wardens everyone who isn’t a Silvan out of the Northern Dominance, they feel its making the situation worse and might allow them the ability to negotiate with the Empress,” he said and before Marcus could speak he held up his hand. “The Empress’s army is already marching, they don’t understand this means she will invade regardless, I need you to talk to the council Marcus, your voice will carry weight,” he said, and the mage sat back regarding the old General. “General Durass, what sway could I possibly have over your people, I have been hidden away down here, hell I haven’t even seen my friends much less many of your people,” Marcus said and again Durass winced. “There was a reason for that, among my people there is a legend, one that pertains to your situation and word has gotten around, you have been kept separate for your protection and frankly to keep you out of the public eye, plus you were needed here and what you needed was here,” Durass answered.

Marcus regarded the general for a moment trying to form words for a problem he never imagined he would have to deal with “What kind of legend?” the Mage finally asked and the General looked embarrassed and with a breath began to speak but doing his best not to make eye contact with Marcus “There is a story of one of the fallen who will arrive when he is most needed, he will be a powerful mage and lead all of our people into the next age,” he said “There is more and its very detailed but suffice it to say, the story of your arrival and the fact the Empress is hunting you has inspired people to believe you are this person” he finished and Marcus stared unbelieving at the other man. “You do understand that your first ones were not sent here by gods and my late arrival probably had a lot more to do with a software glitch then anything else,” he said exasperated. Durass looked at him curiously “Software?” he finally asked, and Marcus shook his head, he had forgotten for a moment who he was talking to “Machinery broke down” he finally chose as the closest thing he could think to associate it with. Durass nodded “I understand and I also know people are desperate and scared and sometimes when they are, they cling to irrational hope Marcus, but because of that it allows you leverage with our council, will you please speak to them, argue for the lowlanders to remain” he finally asked his voice wasn’t quite pleading but the sentiment was there.

Marcus stared at the old General for the moment “Yes General I will, because to turn them away would be tant amount to murder and I can’t condone that” he finished and watched as the General slumped with relief. Marcus thought for a moment and then finally decided there really would never be a good time to ask “General, what do you know of your origins, I mean how it is your people ended up so different from the low landers,” he asked the General, to his credit Durass smiled humorously “I do know that the first ones, a group of them, left behind those like them and came north. They were experts in manipulating aspects of the body and set to work making a new breed of man, one who was more acclimated to their environment, because of the gifts given to the first ones, they studied and researched using the gift to help these changes along, that is how we came to be and the other races as well” Durass finished and Marcus perked up “the other races?” he asked he had not known there were others who had been changed and his mind tried to imagine what other races there could be. Durass smiled again “Some of our animals were augmented and other groups of people continued north, settling in various areas and the first went to work ensuring that they were acclimated to the environment” he finished waving his hand. It appeared that was all Marcus was going to get out of him on the subject, so he concentrated on eating and they spent the rest of the lunch in a companionable silence.

After lunch Marcus returned to his craft table and looked over the armor and components, with a sigh he reached into a drawer and extracted a set of fine tools, etching tools used by the mages to engrave. Marcus closed his eyes, taking a breath he opened them, concentrating on one of the shafts of the mage staff, his eyes began to glow as runic script began to superimpose itself over the object allowing him to mentally see a pattern. With that Marcus gently picked up one of the diamond bit tools and began to etch into the shaft of the weapon. It took many long hours before the entire staff was contoured with runic etchings, once that was complete he pulled a small bowl and a few powered and liquid ingredients and began to mix them together it didn’t take long to complete the liquid and once done he concentrated on it a line of the liquid lifting out of the bowl, it began to run through the indent engraved into the shaft filling it, as it complete a rune and began to flow to the next one, Marcus willed heat into it hardening the rune and then the connector. He did this over the next hour and once he was done, he held up the completed shaft, willing a small amount of power through it and watching as the single runic script over the hilt pulsed with energy.

It was almost dawn when he laid down the last piece on the table, every component covered with runic script. Marcus looked over the pieces laying out in front of him and began picking up pieces from a memory he didn’t own and assembling them. By the time everything was assembled and laid out on the table it was almost time to meet the council. Taking off the cloth shirt and pants he pulled on the black leather pants, the padding giving it the look of motorcycle pants he had owned when he was a teenage, he thought with a smile.

The tall boots were next, the hard soles and multiple buckles took a few minutes to close but once done he walked around the room realizing they had been made to the exact size of his feet and he smiled approvingly. The shirt was next, it was cotton and laced up the front and, on the sleeves, the high neck almost giving it a clerical look as he laced it up and checked the fit. The jacket was next picking it up off of the table he had not realized quite how heavy it was. He held it out and admired the floor length black leather coat with silver buckles, the neck also high again giving it almost an asian look he thought pulling it on and checking the fit. He buttoned up the coat and pulled on the gloves, the knuckles of the black gloves had been reinforced under the leather with metal giving him a hidden advantage in hand to hand combat. There was no focus rune on the glove as he now had one embedded into his skin. He stretched out and moved around, appreciating the fit of the outfit.

Finally, he picked up the obsidian staff, at only three feet long it looked more like a stick he thought, the ends capped with silver and crystal on each end was a carved focus rune, allowing him to use them like thick wands should he need to. He twisted the shafts and the staff lengthened to five feet. Spinning it he felt the weight and balance and again smiled at the ingenuity of the modified design. He shortened the staff back to the original size and then twisted the shafts putting them apart so now he held two foot and a half sticks, the ends could be used to project energy giving him a distance edge in mage to mage combat. He flipped a button and long slender run engraved blades shot out of the other ends, giving him two bladed weapons should he need it.

He retracted the blades and combined the two shafts. On his side was a holster of sorts for the weapon, in fact it was the only weapon he carried. The original design had been modified from the weapon he had seen both Maelen and the Seer carry. The craftsmen had wanted to build him the traditional sword of a Runic Mage, but he had begged off, choosing this instead.

He moved over to the smooth crystal wall and unfolded the cowl on the coat, putting it up he admired himself. The outfit was completely black however the runic script covering every inch of every item he wore was just enough of a color difference to be noticeable. “You are a bad mother fucker” be breathed smiling at himself. He felt dangerous, mysterious it was like when you put on an expensive suit for the first time, or the uniform of the military or police it gave you a feeling of pride and worth. It was like that he thought still admiring the outfit, it was his armor in combat and his protection against those who would judge him he thought.

“I have never seen the armor of a Runic Mage, but I had heard the tales, I have to say seeing it in person, its quite the sight,” Durass said and Marcus could hear the awe in the man’s voice. It must be quite a sight he thought smiling under his cowl “Is it time?” he asked and the General nodded. The two walked over to the spiral staircase that led back up to Durass’s study, Marcus took one last look around he wasn’t sure why be he thought this might be the last time he would see the place. The ancient mage was nowhere to be seen and he felt a pang of regret not being able to thank him.

The two ascended the stairs coming back into the General’s house. “Once we go in there, you will have to speak, I have been censured but my opinion is well known,” he said and Marcus shot a glance at him “Censured?” he asked and Durass shot him a weak smile, one of the costs of gaining you admittance to talk to the council, there were some who were dead set against it.” He finished and motioning to Marcus he led the way out of the room and towards the front of the house.

Durass opened the door and stepped outside, Marcus followed close behind squinting as it took a moment for his eyes to adjust to the light, how long had he been down there he wondered as his vision returned and his eyes widened, looking out over the street it was packed with people all turned towards the General’s porch as everyone pushed one another to get a view of Marcus. “I warned you,” Durass said nodding to soldiers standing to either side of his gate, they began to push and shove people out of the way, though even the shoving was gentle Marcus noticed as Durass began to walk forward and Marcus followed, his cowl up he hid his face from the assault of adulation certainly these people couldn’t believe in a legend he thought or that they would believe he was some kind of chosen figure his mind continued as people reached out, dragging their hands over his armor, trying to touch him.

Though the streets were packed and soldiers had to open the way, the people were orderly, he half expected to be mowed down by a wave of people pushing back against the soldiers, but they aloud themselves to be moved and a path opened as the two walked towards the large government building. Approaching Marcus heard the yells from the Silvan’s, desperate calls of “Save Us” or “Help Us” or “Defend Us” as they continued to walk.

Marcus did not know how to answer, but wanted to explain he wasn’t a legend or a mythic figure but a man, one with fears and doubts like any other but he did not know how to talk to these people, not in a way that they would listen, so he continued forward, his head hidden under the cowl as they made the steps and quickly ascended them. The doors opened and both men stepped inside. As Marcus entered, he turned just in time to not get bowled over as Dara flung herself into his arms.

“Marcus, we were so worried, are you okay?” she asked, pulling away and looking him over, he couldn’t help but smile at her overt show of worry and affection Master Patrice stood just behind her smiling and shaking her head “Its good to see you in one piece Marcus, Durass assured us you were ok, but Dara was worried sick” She finished and he nodded to her, extracting himself from Dara long enough to give Patrice a hug as well. “It is so good to see you two, there is a lot to tell you, but right now I need to see the council,” he said and both women sobered up. “Marcus, they are threatening to send us back across the border, despite the army marching here right now, we would be put directly into their path, they might as well just kill us now” Patrice said bitterly. “The General would never allow that” came a booming voice from his left and Marcus turned to see a large man in Wardens dress, shadowed by an attractive woman, though standing next to him she looked positively tiny. “Uri knows me too well,” Durass said smiling as he walked over and embraced the large man.

“Master Mage Marcus Gavin, this is Master Adjudicator Uri Ammital and his protégé Warden Corliss Bor” The General said and Marcus stepped over, grasping the man’s forearm “It is good to meet you Uri” and then he turned to the woman and cocked his head “Bor?” he asked and she almost blushed as she nodded. “The only daughter of Galen Bor” she almost whispered and then before Marcus could speak “We weren’t close, at all” and grasped his forearm “It is good to meet you Marcus, though I was chasing you for a while for Galen, until you vanished south of Turen,” she said and smiled. Marcus cocked his head; he had no idea anyone had been hunting his and Maelen’s trail but damn if the old man hadn’t been cunning as well as smart if he managed to lose a Master Warden.

Marcus nodded to her “Well I am very glad you did lose me Warden, now if you two don’t mind I am going to try and talk some sense into the council if I can,” he said sounding much more confident then he felt. What the hell do I know about speaking to anyone he thought taking a breath, what the hell can I say to them I am no politician or world leader, Uri or Durass would be far better at this then I would he continued.

He stepped forward trying to hide his nervousness as he stood before the large double doors, the guards motioning him to pause and he did. Laying back the cowl he closed his eyes and mentally braced himself trying to push down the fear trying to well up inside.

The hall stood quiet as Marcus continued to stand upon the threshold of the council’s chambers, the soldiers waiting for orders to allow him admittance. Standing there his mind raced trying to figure out what he would say, how he would say it, knowing the entire future of every low lander and warden rested upon his shoulders and he felt powerless… Ironic he thought, he had been transformed into one of the most powerful mages on the planet and yet here he stood feeling helpless and scared. It was almost enough to make him laugh.

An assistant came out of a side entrance, walking casually over to where Marcus stood, he smiled and nodded “They will see you now Master Mage,” he whispered as the doors opened and light spilled in highlighting the room. Marcus stepped inside the cavernous area was intimidating as he made his way to the center of a semi circle where twelve people sat upon raised platforms looking down upon him. He pushed down the fear and doubt that threatened to overtake him, doing his best to control his breathing he came to stand in the center of the large room, putting his hands behind his back mostly so no one could see them shake Marcus put a small grin on his face and did his best to look relaxed.

“Master Mage Marcus Gavin, thank you for accepting our summons to appear” an elderly lady said from the center podium her face looked kind but Marcus could see the hard penetrating stare of her eyes, calculating even he thought and woe anyone who thought of her as an old doddering fool he mused bowing slightly to the woman as she finished speaking.

“Thank you for having me, I am unsure of protocol so I apologize if I do not address you properly Madam,” he said keeping his face relaxed and the small smile in place despite the nervous energy that now exploded in his body. The woman smiled approvingly “House Chair Master Mage, you may refer to me as Madam or Madam Chairperson” she corrected and again Marcus slightly bowed “Of Course Madam Chairperson, how may I be of service today,” Marcus queried and there were whispered up and down the line of desks he couldn’t hear what exactly they were saying but whatever it was irritated the Chair and she banged a metal ball against her table. “Everyone will have time to ask pertinent questions to the Master Mage,” she stated adding emphasis to pertinent which Marcus found interesting.

“Now, Master Mage the reason we are here is to decide whether to allow all Low Landers the ability to stay within our borders, or to expel them in an effort to stave off the impending attack. There are some here who believe by expelling the LowLanders and the Wardens we will show the Empress we are not a threat nor do we want to get involved in the conflict, there are others who think that if the Empress wished to talk, she would have done so already and expelling refugees back across the border only to have them slaughtered would be a stain on our honor” she finished and judging by the way she had said that Marcus could guess which side she was on. In fact, as he looked up and down the row of desks, he could see the averted gazes, hostile looks or approving nods. Surprisingly there were a lot more of those who seemed to warm to the idea of expelling Marcus thought doing a quick count in his head. If they voted now, he judged it would be close, so there was still hope at least he mused.

Marcus nodded and remembered his conversation with Rogan, the Seer something he had said, something about mundanes and then the words came to him and he began to speak.

“Governors, I have spoken to one of the Empress’s chosen, I am even hunted by her, wanted for some dark purpose. This is a woman who has turned her entire kingdom inside out based on the believe that the entire world should be under one rule and that rule should be by the will of Mages, mundanes only exist according to her doctrine to serve the Mages. Everyone to her is a slave, she has proven this with her action and her disregard for life, The Empress has broken every tenant, pact or promise that the crown has made.” Marcus began, looking over the Governors gathered before him and seeing the different reactions, but despite some of the hostile looks everyone was listening he thought.

“She has not reached out and opened dialog, instead the Empress has marched her army north do you honestly think if you give up her subjects, she will see this as anything other than a sign of weakness and fear spurning her armies here faster?” Marcus asked and could see some of them wince oh so they hadn’t thought of it like that he mused hiding a smile. “Throwing out those who would only be slaughtered as an example of what happens when you defy the Empress as she attempted to the north with those refugees who left your territory and sought safety will be seen as a weak attempt to stave off the inevitable and an affirmation that she is stronger than you” he finished.

“That’s absolute conjecture, he is not a member of the royal court and has no idea what this Empress thinks” one man, standing his massive belly hung over the table as he bellowed more objections, but the Chair quickly silenced him. “Quiet Kopril, you seem to be more them comfortable telling us what she is thinking and the Master Mage spoke to one of her chosen, which is more than you have,” she stated and Marcus could hear the suppressed chuckles up and down the row. “Certainly there has to be some way to reason with her, do we want the bloodshed of our people on our hands without even trying to open a dialog?” came another voice, this one getting more than a few grunts of affirmation. Marcus nodded “That’s a reasonable thought Governor, have you tried to send a messenger to talk to the Seer’s, you might consider trying that to find out if the Empress would even want to entertain terms before you give away your biggest bargaining chip,” Marcus said, he almost winced as he did, where the hell had that come from.

But from wherever in his mind it had flowed it had been the right thing to say, it aloud those that hated the idea a moment to pause and those for it a reason to find out if it would even matter.

Everyone began to whisper to one another until the chair banged the large silver ball quieting them. “Master Mage you speak wisdom beyond that tied to the emotional connection to your low landers and its wisdom I think we can consider, you have given us a few things to think about, would you please wait outside while we deliberate, but please stay close in case we need to call upon you” the chair stated and Marcus bowed deeply “Of Course Madam Chair, I am at your service,” he said turning and walking from the room. As soon as the doors closed everyone gathered looked to him “Well, what are they going to do?” Dara asked and Marcus could see everyone leaning in awaiting an answer.

“They aren’t going to kick us out quite yet, if I had to guess, they are going to send a messenger under a flag of truce to see if the Empress will discuss terms” he finished and there was a reserved sigh of relief. “So, we are not quite out of the woods, but also not having to pack our bags just yet” Uri Ammital said smiling at Marcus “Well done Master Mage, well done” he finished clapping Marcus on the back.

The Governors deliberated for two more hours before the door opened and a tiny woman shuffled out with the aid of a cane, Marcus almost didn’t recognize her, until he saw her eyes “Madam Chair?” he asked confused as to why she would be outside the chambers. Stopping the woman regarded Marcus for a moment and then smiled “Master Mage, will you accompany me for a walk?” she asked and confused Marcus nodded walking over to the woman and stepping next to her keeping up as she shuffled down a hallway, Marcus in tow.

They were silent for a while, going down one hall, turning and going down yet another. The warm wood coloring and feel of the building putting Marcus at ease, as if the natural wood coloring pleased some primal portion of his brain. “Well thanks to your advice, we are going to send a missive to the army marching at us, we will fly under a banner of truce to see if we can open a dialog,” she said smiling up at him. “I wanted to thank you Master Mage, I do not think my ancestors would look kindly upon me throwing innocent people to the wolves so to speak and it wasn’t a decision I was relishing having to make” he finished and he could see the haunted look in the woman’s eyes as she continued.

“I was the one who approved the low lander refugees request to leave, in fact I did it and breathed a sigh a relief that, that political headache was off of my plate. But then I heard what happened, had I thought with my heart Master Mage there would be many more people alive today” she finished, and Marcus nodded. “You made a decision based on the information you had, you could not have known the Empress would seek them out despite them being out of the way of the army and their objective. Now at least you can find out where you stand before making a decision that will not only affect your conscience but also your country” Marcus concluded, and the old woman stopped looking up at the Mage. “Again, you speak wisdom far beyond your years young one, I for one am glad you do,” she said padding Marcus on the arm as they walked back towards the antechamber.

“Master Mage, we are sending a small contingent in the next hour, would you please go and offer any help or advice you can, I don’t expect you to ride out, but having you there” she began and then paused choosing her words “would make a lot of people breathe easier” she finished and smiled up at him. Marcus thought this had something to do with the hero worship outside, but if it helped, he would do it. “Of course, Madam Chair, I would be honored” he concluded and bowed to her as she entered the chamber, the large doors closing behind her.

An hour later Marcus, Dara, Patrice, Durass, Uri and Corliss as well as half of the Governors and their staff stood in the woodline as six riders on horses flew a white flag as they rode across the field. The soldiers had been on the other side of the clearing since the morning, they had made no move to advance but also had not setup any camps, Marcus didn’t know what they were waiting for and for some reason it was making him nervous as the center of his shoulder blades itched, as if someone had painted a target on it.

“I am surprised they are riding horses General; I would have thought you would want to show your enemy what awaits them,” Uri said, and Marcus cocked his head straining to hear the conversation over the rustling of trees. Durass smiled “We decided to keep that for a surprise, besides you Low Landers brought so many horses, why waste them and tire our beasts out,” he said. What beasts Marcus wondered as he watched the riders slow as they approached the standing army.

They slowed coming to stand directly in front of two robed figures with their cowls drawn. Marcus tried to get a better look at them but couldn’t, they were Lord Seer’s he knew that much, but would Rogan be here he wondered. After only a moment, one of the robed figures stood on his horse and made a motion with his hand. Everyone gasped in horror as flame engulfed the riders and the horses, the animals died so fast they didn’t even take a step before collapsing. Marcus couldn’t hear the men scream, but he knew what happened when you covered a man in magic flame as he thought back to Orah’s farm and the collectors.

Before anyone could react, a call went up and to Marcus’s horror as he watched a hundred blue energy trails erupted from behind the line of soldiers. “Cannons” Yelled Uri and everyone began to scramble, Marcus turned to the group gaining everyone’s attention by clapping his hands and enhancing it with magic, what it produced was as loud as a thunder clap, everyone jumping but now looking at him “get everyone back to the city, I will cover you,” he said evenly and then turned back to the oncoming enemy fire. He stepped just outside of the treeline and looked up as the arcs reached their pinnacle and began to descend on his position.

He watched them for a few moments, the cannons had been loaded and aimed well in advance, as if the enemy knew this is something the Silvan’s would do and that they would have some of their leaders here watching, they had hoped to cut the head of the snake Marcus thought shaking his head as anger welled up in him. “Not today,” he said and summoned energy, the runes over his body flowing faster as they accumulated massive amounts of power, his eyes flashed blue as he pushed towards the enemy with his hand and the cannon fire snapped back, the energy trails reversing as the balls returned home to their guns.

He could here the yelling once the soldiers saw the incoming fire and a moment later satisfying explosions over the horizon as they landed among the artillery and the soldiers who manned them. One of the Robed seers rode forward dropping his cowl and Marcus’s blood boiled as Rogan leered at him from across the field. The Seer had the sheer audacity to wave at Marcus and then turn his horse and casually return to the line. Taking a step he felt the anger for this man well up inside him, remembering Maelen and what he had done to him.

Taking another step, he pointed to the Seer “Rogan, I am coming for you,” Marcus yelled and even from across the field he could see the man smile. Then from his left he heard the heavy beat of horse hoofs and turned to see four of the Empress’s soldiers bearing down on him, two held staves and a net was drawn out between the others. Did they really think to capture him he thought shaking his head? Marcus pushed at the next and realized that he couldn’t move it, thinking quickly he knocked the feet out from under the horses instead, sending them crashing to the ground as their riders spilled and rolled, coming to land directly in front of Marcus.

Panicing the Soldiers got up and began to run across the field, back towards the line. Reaching out he grabbed one with his power and snapped him back to land at his feet, the others he aloud to leave, for now. Marcus summoned power and lifted the man off the ground, levitating him so his face was parallel with Marcus’s “What did Rogan do with the body of the Mage he killed in the village of short shore?” he asked. The man was frantically searching for a way out, Marcus tightened his power around him squeezing the soldier and he could hear him fight for breath. “Answer me” he growled when this was done, Marcus would see Maelen buried with honors he thought.

Then the soldier spoke the shock of his words freezing Marcus in place for a moment. “The mage he captured. He is not buried, Rogan had him taken to the tower, please, please don’t kill me” the soldier stammered. Captured Marcus thought, thinking back to the battle, he had heard the explosions and the screams, but he had never seen a body, instead he had assumed Maelen dead and just left his friend. She knew how to alter her chosen, not because she had gotten a book or learned a long-lost secret he thought. # Epilogue: Holy Ruler

Holy Ruler

“We return this land to order and prosperity, we bring to it a balanced rule, a stewardship until such time that we are able to return a ruler who is worthy of your obedience, we return the nobility to their lands and bring back the rules and dictates of the Emperor, the true ruler of this land. We the Messenger s of Aurtus will ensure continuity until a new Emperor or Empress is found.” The Lord Regent announced to the gathered people of Vicor, the entire commons were packed with nobles and common alike as a cheer rose up to greet his words. The Lord Regent spoke from the balcony of the palace where the church now ruled.

Nero had returned prosperity, freed the children and shut down the tower. Things were beginning to look like they would come back to normal Marcus thought leaning in the doorway and watching the speech.

Soldiers walked by eyeing his clothing, the outlook towards mages had not improved but at the same time the church had not spoken of the agreement, or their intentions. Marcus hoped for the best and sighed as he turned back towards the room where His friends sat Corliss now the Lord Adjudicator of the new Wardens stood resplendent in her new leather armor complete with the crest of the wardens emblazoned upon it, Uri having retired to live in peace with the Silvan’s, Dara, Maelen and Patrice now sat around the small table listening as the Regent continued to speak of prosperity and peace.

“So, what’s next?” Maelen asked looking towards Marcus who shifted uncomfortably his friend had seen the packed back by the door and knew what that meant. “I am heading North, there is a lot to see and this place holds too many bad memories for now,” he said and could see Dara’s face fall. “You’re leaving?” she asked coming over to him and Marcus took the young mage by the shoulders. “There is something out there, something I can’t explain the Empress said some things to me that leads me to believe she saw it too,” he said lowly “I am going to find out what I can, I will be back, but there is a lot that I need to find out” he finished.

The speech ended to applause and Marcus nodded to his friends. Corliss came over first “Look me up when you come back Marcus, a lot of whats going on today is because of you, for that you have my thanks,” she said giving him a hug and then stepping back. Maelen walked him out “Where will you go, back to the Silvan’s?” he asked walking with his friend as he left the small house and headed towards the stables. Marcus was quiet for a few minutes and then looked at his friend “Not sure, I think I am going to find out what else is out there, Durass spoke of other people further north, I want to see if they might know something about these dreams,” he said. His friend was quiet for a long time. “When you come back, we should have the order back, if the new ruler allows it,” he said wincing, knowing how Nero felt about the mages.

“Take care my friend, I will see you when I get back,” Marcus said giving his friend a hug and then walking on, leaving Maelen looking after him as Marcus mounted the large cat that stood outside the stables, since the stable boy wouldn’t go near the large animal.

He rode out of the capital and headed north, looking back at the capital he lowered his head and whispered a small prayer for his friends and then turned heading into the setting sun.
