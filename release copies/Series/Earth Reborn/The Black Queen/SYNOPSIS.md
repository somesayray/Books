# The Black Queen
## Earth Reborn Book Two

**Author:** Raymond Bayly  
**Working structure:** Prologue and 12 chapters  
**Status:** Adopted trilogy division, 23 October 2026

## Reader-facing description

The Empress’s rule leaves little room for mercy. Lord Seer Rogan hunts the man she wants while tightening his grip on the people already under his command.

Marcus Gavin needs allies—and a better understanding of the power that has kept him alive. Among the Silvans, he finds knowledge that may let him face the crown. Elsewhere, Nero gathers the dispossessed under the Church’s protection, carrying a hatred that grows with every atrocity he witnesses.

As resistance takes shape, Marcus and the refugees discover how little safety the borders of an empire can offer.

## Editorial synopsis — spoilers

Rogan enforces the Empress’s demands through exemplary violence and interrogation. Marcus’s search for knowledge takes him into Silvan society, where his developing relationship with runic power offers a means of resisting the crown.

The emerging resistance is divided in its aims. The Wardens seek practical alliances, while Nero works among the displaced and increasingly blames those with the gift for the violence he has witnessed. Refuge, political authority, and the survival of the people under his protection become inseparable concerns.

The volume follows these developments through the three Check and Mate chapters. Its final sequence follows Nero and the refugees to an abandoned Warden refuge. Their apparent safety ends when creatures attack. Nero kills one with his bare hands, and the others withdraw. His realization that the creatures can be killed is inseparable from the hatred overtaking him.

## Editorial boundary

This volume now ends after Check and Mate (Part III). The final military campaign, confrontation with the Empress, and Holy Ruler epilogue belong exclusively to Fall of the Empire.

A closing scene returns to Marcus in Silvan. Reflecting on Orah and the danger of becoming like the Seer, he turns to the designs on his table and the work of protecting the people around him. It leads into Book Three’s workshop opening. The full manuscript line edit remains in progress.
