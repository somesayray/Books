# Earth Reborn continuity and copyedit report

23 October 2026

> Historical report of the earlier copyedit. The author subsequently approved the five canon decisions below; see `FULL_LINE_EDIT_STATUS.md` for the current pass. The unresolved decisions in this report no longer represent the author’s instructions.

The adopted Markdown manuscripts received 1,944 logged edits. The 16 / 12 / 8 chapter trilogy division remains intact, with Mortis retained as the 26-chapter prequel. The final campaign and Holy Ruler remain exclusively in Book Three.

The author directed that the Queen’s incompatible origin accounts be preserved and flagged. They remain unresolved. The chronology also contains incompatible elapsed-time statements; this pass does not certify the series as publication-ready.

## Copyediting applied

The pass scanned all four current manuscript files for spelling candidates, dialogue attribution punctuation, name variants, comparative and possessive homophones, and draft residue. Spelling and homophone candidates were checked in their immediate narrative context before correction. A constrained punctuation rule supplied missing punctuation before explicit speech attributions; it did not automatically split paragraphs or infer changes of speaker throughout the books.

The changes include 320 spelling repairs, 150 there/their corrections, 50 then/than comparisons, and 1,162 dialogue punctuation repairs. Locative uses of there were retained. The chronological phrase “more then deciding” was excluded from the comparative replacement. The jail’s “tenants” were retained while references to doctrines became “tenets.” Lostric as a campaign name was not turned into the character Postric.

Proper-name capitalization was regularized. The mage traveling with Nero is now consistently Korlin where the text previously used Kolrim or Kolrin. This is an editorial spelling choice supported by Book Two’s usage and the indexed beta note naming Nero, Korlin, and Jorun; it does not identify the dying Regent Cholrin or the separately mentioned Colrin as that mage. Those distinct references remain untouched.

Selected prose received closer revision: Marcus’s awakening now separates perception, physical discomfort, and recalled instructions; Book Three’s workshop opening gives returning readers a clearer account of his new abilities; Book Two’s final attack has clearer action and sentence boundaries. The events and outcomes of these passages are preserved. No new closing scene for Marcus was invented.

## Continuity repairs applied

- **Rogan and Nero:** Rogan’s first name is Juric; Nero’s is Veric. Reversed full names and three residual Veric references inside Rogan’s viewpoint were corrected. References to the actual Messenger Veric were preserved.
- **Marcus and Galen:** the person reacting to news that Corliss had pursued Marcus and Maelen is Marcus. The mistaken Galen attribution was corrected.
- **Third-person viewpoint:** a stray “he shot me a look” in the cat scene now names Marcus as the recipient.
- **Corvus in Mortis:** the vault attack no longer apparently kills him before his later appearance. He tears the construct from his helmet and retreats; his later death remains in place. This is a short continuity bridge, recorded in the diff.
- **The unopened vault:** Arianna’s earlier line now says the way to the vault is clear. It no longer announces that the vault is already open before the later tablet-and-door sequence.
- **The shield and chamber:** the constructs strike the shield, avoiding a contradictory switch from steel to oak and bronze; Corvus falls against the chamber’s blood-slicked stone rather than unexplained mire.
- **Historical era:** the coronation begins a new reign. It no longer declares that the Second Age itself begins there, despite centuries of earlier post-collapse history.
- **Draft residue:** the stray alternate labels “Sic Pac Parabellum” and “Forced in Either” were removed beneath the adopted chapter headings. A malformed Markdown scene divider was repaired.

## Open continuity register

### C01 — The Queen’s origin — preserve both accounts

Mortis, chapter eleven, presents Arianna as the programmer’s descendant and intended interface with Mortis. Fall of the Empire, chapter eight, presents Victoria/Eleneaor as an ancient commander recalling her own confrontation with Tokimada and Zathris. These are incompatible without an explanation of identity, memory, or unreliable testimony.

**Author decision:** flag the conflict and preserve both accounts. No alias history, reincarnation, or inherited-memory mechanism has been invented. This remains a deliberate unresolved editorial query, not a resolved mystery.

### C02 — Time since the ARC exodus — decision needed

The Fallen, chapter one, supplies several different scales:

- Sentinel says twelve pods returned over three centuries.
- Sentinel then says “Two hundred twelve years since exodus.”
- Marcus subsequently thinks he is ten centuries late.
- The awakening refers to a millennium without sunlight.

The ancient-war history in Book Three also spans roughly a thousand years. Mortis gives its installation a five-century history. These cannot all be treated as one settled elapsed-time scale without distinguishing the events being dated. Sentinel’s restricted information may be intentional, but the text does not yet make that a sufficient explanation.

**Recommendation:** choose explicit dates for the initial departure, first mass return, ancient mage conflict, and Marcus’s return. Then revise both the machine’s statements and Marcus’s deductions together. This pass preserves the disputed figures, including the millennium reference in the revised awakening.

### C03 — Corliss’s full name — decision needed

The Fallen, chapter twelve, explicitly identifies Lorin as her first name and introduces her as Lorin Corliss. Book Three chapters one and two use Corliss Bor. The text does not establish whether Bor is an alias, an additional family name, or an older draft variant.

**Working register:** Lorin Corliss, she/her. **Unresolved variant:** Corliss Bor. Neither form has been silently removed. The Bor surname is also used for Galen, which makes an unsupported global replacement especially undesirable.

### C04 — Re-entry and geographic linkage — clarification needed

Mortis’s final scene places the detected activity over the southern ice sheets and has the Queen name Marcus before meeting him. The Fallen’s opening has Sentinel discuss the North American continent and guides Marcus toward a western supply bunker. An observed orbital trajectory need not equal a landing site, so these details are not automatically contradictory. The narrative should make the relationship clear if the prequel’s signal is meant to be the same arrival.

**Status:** preserved. Do not relocate the landing or invent a transcontinental journey during copyediting.

### C05 — Book Two’s ending — developmental consideration

The adopted ending resolves Nero’s immediate confrontation with the creature and sharpens his radicalization. It does not provide a final beat for Marcus’s viewpoint. This pass improves the existing scene without changing the approved boundary or adding a new plot event. A later developmental revision can address the viewpoint balance.

## Scope and verification

The entire adopted Markdown corpus was mechanically scanned. Close reading concentrated on flagged passages, character-identity contexts, the prequel’s action sequence, opening and closing transitions, and continuity-bearing statements. This is a substantial copyediting pass, not a claim that every sentence received an exhaustive human-style line edit. Long dialogue paragraphs, comma splices, capitalization of common titles, and scene pacing still warrant a subsequent line-by-line editorial review.

Every recorded change was replayed from the saved pre-pass text. The replay matches each edited file exactly. Chapter headings and order are unchanged; no entire chapter was deleted or duplicated by this pass. The original manuscripts and binary editions were not edited. Existing DOCX and PDF releases remain out of sync with both the adopted structure and this copyedit.

The prior proposed-trilogy files are historical structural references, not the latest copyedited masters. Use the manuscript Markdown in each book’s release folder for further work.
