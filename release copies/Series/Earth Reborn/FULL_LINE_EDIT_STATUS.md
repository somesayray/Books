## Author-approved canon implemented

1. Marcus returns **212 years after the ARC exodus**. The main return is approximately two centuries before his arrival. Conflicting millennium, three-century pod-release, and five-century Mortis references have been revised. The Mage Wars and Agreement are placed approximately 150 years before Marcus’s return, retaining the earlier manuscript’s century-and-a-half reference.
2. The Queen is **Arianna, descendant of Mortis’s programmer**. She merges with Mortis thirty years before Marcus’s arrival. Book Three’s reveal replaces the ancient-commander and buried-pod account; Victoria Augustus is the identity under which she rules in Vicor after marrying its emperor. She is not Eleneaor recalling her own ancient battles.
3. **Lorin Corliss** is the canonical full name. Her relationship to her father Galen Bor remains intact.
4. Mortis’s southern detection is an **orbital trajectory**, with descent projected over North America. A passenger record carried by the beacon supplies Marcus’s name.
5. Book Two has a **closing Marcus character scene** after Nero’s attack. Marcus reflects on Orah, the danger of using power like the Seer, and his original purpose as a builder. It leads to Book Three’s workshop without giving him premature knowledge of the refugees’ attack.

These decisions are applied. The remaining full trilogy read must still check their implications across all scenes, including the relationship between Eldoria’s earlier dominion and Vicor’s later politics.
