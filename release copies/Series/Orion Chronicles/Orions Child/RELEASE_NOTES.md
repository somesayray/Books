## Canonical Continuity & Character Alignment

### 1. Protagonist Profile: Gracie Campbell & "The Box"
- **Origin**: Follows directly from the cliffhanger epilogue of *Vengeance* (Book 3).
- **The Box & The Garbage Chute Escape**:
  - Captured by Vengeance, Gracie had an illegal obsidian biomechanical containment canister ("The Box") grafted into her brainstem.
  - Encased within is the digitized consciousness of **a prisoner tortured by Vengeance for an entire century (100 years)**.
  - **The Breakout**: She doesn't kill anyone. Left alone on the gurney for a moment following the surgery, Gracie painfully drags herself across the deck to an automated garbage chute, falling into a space-faring trash truck / waste scow as it undocks into space. She passes out into the dark.
  - **Three Months Later**: The narrative jumps three months forward. Gracie has survived the trash scow, treated her wounds, acquired *The Starlight Drifter*, and gathered her crew, while constantly wrestling with the century-tortured passenger in her skull.
  - The entity suffers from severe PTSD, erratic panic attacks, and paranoia. While possessing a century of combat knowledge, **the Box doesn't always give the best advice**, forcing Gracie to constantly filter tactical insights from trauma-driven madness.
- **Crew of The Starlight Drifter**:
  - **Kaelen 'Kael' Voss**: Ex-consortium defense engineer with a hydraulic titanium arm; builds a quantum stabilizer to help Gracie ease the Box's agonizing feedback.
  - **Tessandra 'Tess' Renn**: Street-smart hacker and drone specialist.
  - **Unit-7**: De-shackled combat synthetic providing heavy fire support with deadpan humor.

### 2. Narrative Arc Summary
- **Prologue (*The Chute*)**: Vengeance's black site surgery, the painful crawl to the garbage chute, falling into the space trash truck, and the fade out into blackness.
- **Act I (Ch 1-4, 3 Months Later)**: The contract at Freeport 9 cantina, grappling with the Box's frantic warnings and bad advice, navigation into the Obsidian Nebula, and salvage of the ancient precursor *Aegis Core*.
- **Act II (Ch 5-11)**: Ambush by Baron Vargas's Black Sun Cartel, emergency fracture jump, psychic bleed revealing the prisoner's century of torture and Gracie's garbage chute escape, New Tortuga refinery betrayal by Jax (prompted by bad advice from the Box), and surviving an assault by the Shadow AI Remnant.
- **Act III (Ch 12-16)**: Escorting civilian refugee transports, boarding *The Black Leviathan*, channeling the Box's century of stored trauma and her raw will to survive into an offensive neural burst to defeat Baron Vargas, and cementing her status as the *Child of Orion*.
