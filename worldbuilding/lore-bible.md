# Lore Bible: World & Setting Reference

## Setting Overview
The world exists at the convergence of high-technology cognitive substrates and analog frontier landscapes. Isolated bastions of archival knowledge survive in hardened vaults, linked only by low-frequency quantum relays.

## Core Factions
1. **The Archivists**: Keepers of text records and unadulterated knowledge. They reject ephemeral virtual fronts in favor of immutable text documents.
2. **The Grid Runners**: Operators who maintain the transport networks and control towers (Air Traffic Control).
3. **The Synthetics**: Autonomous reasoning entities bound by strict zero-trust governance protocols.

## Core Directives
- **Immutability of Text**: Knowledge is preserved in raw text documents, readable across generations without reliance on volatile web frontends.
- **Root Protocol**: Author-MCP acts as the single source of truth and coordination hub across all subsectors.
